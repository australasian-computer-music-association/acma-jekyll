## **Synaesthetica '94**

1–3 July 1994, Australian Centre for the Arts and Technology (ACAT), Australian National University, Canberra, Australia

The 1994 ACMA symposium was organised at ACAT by David Worrall. It opened with Barry Jones launching the exhibition *Contours of the Mind*, "a celebration of Fractals, Chaos and Feedback", followed by a performance by ACAT staff and students. The weekend had five paper sessions, two concerts, an open forum and a plenary.

### **Proceedings**

Registration included "a copy of the proceedings" ([Chroma 17]({% link chroma_pdf/Chroma17.pdf %}), p. 4), so a printed volume was produced. We do not have a copy. If you have one, please [get in touch](mailto:info@computermusic.org.au).
