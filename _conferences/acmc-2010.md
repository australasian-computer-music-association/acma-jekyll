## **Engagement**

The Australian National University, Canberra, Australia, 24–26 June 2010

## Proceedings

*Engagement: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2010*, edited by Timothy Opie. Australasian Computer Music Association, July 2010. ISSN 1448-7780.
Each keynote, paper and artist talk below has its own page with a citation, and so does each work in the five concerts.

[Complete proceedings (PDF)]({% link proceedings/ACMC2010-proceedings.pdf %}) · [Conference website, with the concert programme](https://web.archive.org/web/20110219174826/http://conference.acma.asn.au/ACMC_10.html) (archived copy)

The volume has no concert programme. The concert works below are listed from the programme on the conference website, which gives each work's type, channels and instrumentation but no program notes. The concerts were held in the Band Room, Peter Karmel Building, ANU, at 12 noon and 5:30pm on 24 and 25 June and at 12 noon on 26 June; "Late Night Live" concerts were held at venues around the city. Concert 4 also had *A Concert on Mobiles* (Kimmo, Leah and Ian) and *iPerformance* (various performers), which are not entered as works.
