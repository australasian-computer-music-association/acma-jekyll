## **MAKE!**

University of Technology Sydney, 18–21 November 2015

[Conference website](https://web.archive.org/web/20160302070233/http://acmc2015.net/) (archived copy)<!-- original link, dead by 2026: http://acmc2015.net -->

## Proceedings

*ACMC2015 – MAKE! Proceedings of the Annual Conference of the Australasian Computer Music Association*, edited by Ian Stevenson. Australasian Computer Music Association, November 2015. ISSN 1448-7780.
Each paper, abstract, artist talk and studio report below has its own page with a citation, and so does each work in the concerts.

[Complete proceedings (PDF)]({% link proceedings/ACMC2015-proceedings.pdf %}) · [Abstracts (PDF)]({% link proceedings/ACMC2015-proceedings/ACMC2015-abstracts.pdf %})

The volume has no concert programme. The concert works below are listed from the conference website's day pages ([Wednesday](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730090606/http://acmc2015.net/?page_id=127), [Thursday](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730114645/http://acmc2015.net/?page_id=136), [Friday](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730114635/http://acmc2015.net/?page_id=146), [Saturday](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730054702/http://acmc2015.net/?page_id=154)), which give program notes and biographies for the evening concerts. The Saturday works were played in *Physical Meets Virtual*, a live ABC Classic FM broadcast for Australian Music Month from Studio 227; its line-up is from the Saturday page, which also lists Nigel Helyer's *Biologging_Retrofit* (entered under Concert 2). The site's schedule page lists Luke Harrald's *The Hypnotist* (live performer and interactive electronics) in the broadcast instead, and gives Atherton and Drummond's work no title. The conference also had a Data Arena session (Andrew Bluff and Ben Simons), a listening walk (Sophea Lerner), lunchtime demonstrations in the Interactivation Studio (Rodney Berry, *Tangible Bytebeats*; Bert Bongers; Damian Castaldi, *Play Ongaku*), a NIME 2016 presentation (Andrew Brown) and Cat Hope's keynote, none of which are printed in the volume.
