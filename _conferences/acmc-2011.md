## Proceedings

*Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2011*, edited by John Coulter. Australasian Computer Music Association, August 2011. ISSN 1448-7780.
Each keynote, paper, abstract and artist talk below has its own page with a citation.

[Complete proceedings (PDF)]({% link proceedings/ACMC2011-proceedings.pdf %})

The programme also had three workshops, which aren't in the proceedings: a multichannel workshop (John Coulter and David Rylands), a NOP workshop (Nolwenn Hugain-Lacire) and a taonga pūoro workshop (Jason Phillips). Artist talks by John Cousins (“Witness”) and Jason Phillips weren't printed either.
