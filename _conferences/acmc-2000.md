## **interFACES**

Queensland University of Technology, Kelvin Grove, Brisbane, Australia, 5–8 July 2000

## Proceedings

*Australasian Computer Music Conference 2000: interFACES*, edited by Andrew Brown and Richard Wilding. Australasian Computer Music Conference 2000, Brisbane, 2000. ISBN 0-646-39885-7.
Each full paper, abstract and workshop below has its own page with a citation.

[Complete proceedings (PDF)]({% link proceedings/ACMC2000-proceedings.pdf %})

The volume prints no concert programme. The [conference schedule in *Chroma* 28](https://web.archive.org/web/20030806211355/http://www.acma.asn.au:80/Chroma28.html) (June 2000) lists ten concerts, including a retrospective of La Trobe electroacoustic works curated by David Hirst and live-to-air concerts on 4ZZZ, but it doesn't name the works.
