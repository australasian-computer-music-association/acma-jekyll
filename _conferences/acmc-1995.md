## **ACMA Conference '95**

9–11 June 1995, Faculty of Music, University of Melbourne, Melbourne, Australia

The 1995 conference focused on the composition and aesthetics of electroacoustic music. Paper abstracts were anonymously refereed. It ran from an official opening and evening concert on Friday, through papers and an evening concert on Saturday, to papers and a lunchtime concert on Sunday, and included studio reports from around Australia. The conference coordinators were Lawrence Harvey and Alistair Riddell.

### **Keynote**

- David Jaffe
