## **ACMA Conference 1996**

Brisbane, Australia

Very little is recorded about the 1996 conference. Chroma was not published between May 1995 ([Chroma 20]({% link chroma_pdf/Chroma20.pdf %})) and December 1996 ([Chroma 21]({% link chroma_pdf/Chroma21.pdf %})), and neither issue covers it. In 2000, Warren Burt's editorial in [Chroma 26–27]({% link chroma_pdf/Chroma26-27.pdf %}) recalls "the last ACMA conference in Brisbane, in 1996", organised by Andrew Brown.
