19th – 21st June 2007 The Australian National University Centre for New Media Arts The ANU School of Music Canberra, Australia

## Proceedings

*ACMC07 Trans: Boundaries / Permeability / Reification – Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2007*, edited by Alistair Riddell and Alex Thorogood. Australasian Computer Music Association, June 2007. ISSN 1448-7780.
Each keynote, paper, artist talk, studio report and poster below has its own page with a citation, and so does each work in the six concerts.
