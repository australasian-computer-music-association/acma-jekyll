ACMC 1997

Interface ‘97: computers – music – education

11–13 July 1997, School of Music, University of Auckland, Auckland, New Zealand

Incorporating the ACMA Conference 1997, Interface ‘97 was “the fifth annual conference of the Australian and New Zealand Computer Music community”, and the first held in New Zealand. It was jointly presented by the University of Auckland School of Music and the Australian Computer Music Association. The planned programme had three concerts, six paper sessions, a conference dinner and an open discussion forum.
