ACMC 1999

Imaginary Space

7–10 July 1999, School of Music, Victoria University of Wellington, Wellington, New Zealand

The 1999 conference, organised by John Young, Philip Brownlee and colleagues, had a keynote, six paper sessions, a panel discussion and a festival of concerts drawn from 140 submissions, held in the Adam Concert Room on a 16-channel system. Radio New Zealand’s Concert FM broadcast two programmes of music from the conference. About 45 people took part, including visitors from the USA, Sweden and Britain. Chroma 24 calls it “the 1999 ACMC”, the earliest use of that name we have found.

Keynote

  • Jonty Harrison (University of Birmingham / BEAST), on acousmatic music and diffusion

Proceedings

The printed proceedings held “17 research papers and two studio reports” (Gordon Monro, Chroma 26–27). We do not have a copy. If you have one, please get in touch.

Papers

From the schedule in Chroma 25.

Session 1 (Wednesday 7 July)

  • Andrew R. Brown: Tools and Outcomes: computer music systems and music directions
  • Tim Kreger: Real-time Cellular Automata Filters Implemented with Max MSP
  • Damien Lock: Designing a music composition system in an “open source” environment
  • Ian Fredericks: SPACE_N

Session 2 (Thursday 8 July)

  • Densil Cabrera: The Size of Sound: Auditory Volume Reassessed
  • Gordon Monro: Some sound modifications using wavelets
  • Ian Kaminskyj: Multidimensional Scaling Analysis of Musical Instrument Sound CQT Spectra

Session 3 (Thursday 8 July)

  • Terence McDermott and Ross Bencina: The use of Dynamic Nested Space in the Acousmatic Presentation of Electroacoustic Work
  • Oliver Hancock: Music with the Gestural Qualities of Environmental sounds: Using a Nonlinear Dynamic System as a Physical Metaphor
  • Warren Burt: The Spectacle and Computer Music: A Critical Assessment

Session 4 (Friday 9 July)

  • Mathew Adkins: Acoustic Chains in Electroacoustic Music
  • Michael Norris: Reinstating Interpretation: the status of analysis in an electroacoustic context

Session 5 (Friday 9 July)

  • Andrew Lyons: Time as Space: the spatial/visual abstraction of music in Schwarz Child
  • David Worrall: Cyberspace and Sound
  • Allyson Newman, Jessie Ginsborg-Newling, Sean Bridgeman and Greg Schiemer: Composing with the Lake 3D audio system
  • Jon Drummond: Visualisation of Auditory Space

Session 6 (Saturday 10 July)

  • Greg Schiemer, Narayan Mani and Siri Rama: “Shantivanam 2”: interactive performance using an audio signal processor
  • John Elmsly: Beyond the Sounds….

Studio Reports

  • Anthony Hood: Sydney Conservatorium of Music
  • Martin Wesley-Smith and Greg Schiemer: Electronic Music Studio, Sydney Conservatorium of Music

Concerts

The full concert listing (nine concerts, three late-night concerts and installations) is in Chroma 25. Installations were by Garth Paine (Map 1), Jessie Ginsborg-Newling (Inundare) and Reynold Weidenaar (Magic Music from the Telharmonium).
