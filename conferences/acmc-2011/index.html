ACMC 2011

Organic Sounds in Live Electroacoustic Music

School of Music, University of Auckland, New Zealand, 6–9 July 2011

Conference website (archived copy)

Proceedings

Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2011, edited by John Coulter. Australasian Computer Music Association, August 2011. ISSN 1448-7780. Each keynote, paper, abstract and artist talk below has its own page with a citation.

Complete proceedings (PDF)

The programme also had three workshops, which aren’t in the proceedings: a multichannel workshop (John Coulter and David Rylands), a NOP workshop (Nolwenn Hugain-Lacire) and a taonga pūoro workshop (Jason Phillips). Artist talks by John Cousins (“Witness”) and Jason Phillips weren’t printed either.
