ACMC 1996

ACMA Conference 1996

Brisbane, Australia

Very little is recorded about the 1996 conference. Chroma was not published between May 1995 (Chroma 20) and December 1996 (Chroma 21), and neither issue covers it. In 2000, Warren Burt’s editorial in Chroma 26–27 recalls “the last ACMA conference in Brisbane, in 1996”, organised by Andrew Brown.
