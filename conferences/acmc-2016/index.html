ACMC 2016

Sonic Environments

Queensland Conservatorium, Griffith University, Brisbane, 10–11 July 2016. Held with the Australian Forum for Acoustic Ecology, and in collaboration with NIME 2016.

Conference website (archived copy)

Proceedings

Sonic Environments: Proceedings of the 2016 Annual Conference of the Australasian Computer Music Association, edited by Lindsay Vickery. Australasian Computer Music Association, July 2016. ISSN 2206-5296. Each paper, abstract, artist talk and creative work below has its own page with a citation.

Complete proceedings (PDF) · Abstracts and posters (PDF) · Artist talks (PDF) · Creative works (PDF)

Review process and committees

The peer review process applied to full papers published in these proceedings involved impartial and independent assessment and review of the papers before publication, conducted by independent, qualified experts as required by section 6.5 of the 2015 Higher Education Research Data Collection Specifications of the Australian Government Department of Education and Training.
