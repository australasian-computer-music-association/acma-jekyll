ACMC 2000

interFACES

Queensland University of Technology, Kelvin Grove, Brisbane, Australia, 5–8 July 2000

Proceedings

Australasian Computer Music Conference 2000: interFACES, edited by Andrew Brown and Richard Wilding. Australasian Computer Music Conference 2000, Brisbane, 2000. ISBN 0-646-39885-7. Each full paper, abstract and workshop below has its own page with a citation.

Complete proceedings (PDF)

The volume prints no concert programme. The conference schedule in Chroma 28 (June 2000) lists ten concerts, including a retrospective of La Trobe electroacoustic works curated by David Hirst and live-to-air concerts on 4ZZZ, but it doesn’t name the works.
