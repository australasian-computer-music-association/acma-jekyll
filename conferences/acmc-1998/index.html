ACMC 1998

ACMA 98

10–12 July 1998, Canberra School of Music, Australian National University, Canberra, Australia

The “Sixth Annual Conference of the Australasian Computer Music Association” was organised by the Australian Centre for the Arts and Technology (ACAT). The call notes the association’s new name, the Australasian Computer Music Association (previously the Australian Computer Music Association). The proposed schedule had four paper sessions, four concerts, a forum and the ACMA AGM.

Keynote

  • Martin Wesley-Smith (followed by a concert)
