ACMC 2002

Form Space Time: Music Architecture and Design

RMIT University and Victorian College of the Arts, Melbourne, Australia, 6–8 July 2002

Proceedings

Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002. Australasian Computer Music Association, May 2002. Each paper, artist talk, studio report and concert work below has its own page with a citation.

Complete proceedings (PDF) · Programmes from the conference website: papers, Concert 1, Concert 2, Concert 3, Concert 4, Concert 5 (PDF)

The volume prints no programme. The conference website (iii.rmit.edu.au/sonology/ACMC2002/, archived by the Wayback Machine in 2002–03) does: the keynote by Gerard Pape, “Form, Space, Time”, opened the paper sessions on Saturday 6 July in Building 94, RMIT, and five concerts with notes and biographies were held in Room 113 of the VCA School of Music, at 6 pm and 8.30 pm on Saturday 6 and Sunday 7 July and at 6 pm on Monday 8 July. The concert works below come from those programmes, which say nothing about how works were selected. The papers programme also lists John Davies’s Australian Music Centre report, Lawrence Harvey’s studio representatives’ forum report and a closing forum session, none of which the volume prints and none entered. The website’s “web proceedings” held the authors’ own PDFs of most papers; they replace the scans cut from the volume at the same addresses, except for Bajurnow and Uitdenbogerd (abstract only), Bandt and Mott (first page missing) and Whalley (not there).
