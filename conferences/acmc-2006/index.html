ACMC 2006

MEDI(T)ATIONS: computers/music/intermedia

11-14 July 2006 Elder Conservatorium of Music University of Adelaide, Australia

Proceedings

Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2006, edited by Sonia Wilkie and Christian Haines. Australasian Computer Music Association, July 2006. ISSN 1448-7780. Each keynote, paper, poster, studio report, artist talk, workshop, concert work and installation below has its own page with a citation.

Download full proceedings [ZIP, 29 MB, via the Internet Archive] · Conference programme (PDF)

The volume has no programme or front matter. The conference programme from the website does: six concerts in the Scott Theatre at 1.30 pm and 6 pm on Tuesday 11, Wednesday 12 and Thursday 13 July, with 30 works, and six installations in the Asylum (Schulz Building, Level 3) from 9 am to 6 pm on those days. The concert works and installations below come from it, without notes, formats or durations, which the programme doesn’t give. Mitchell Whitelaw’s keynote opened Tuesday (9.45 am), Peter McIlwain’s invited talk followed at 11 am, and klipp av’s keynote closed Thursday’s papers (4.30 pm), all in the Seminar Room on Level 10 of the Schulz Building, where the papers were also given. Not entered: the two morning forums (“History is Junk” on Wednesday and “Medi(t)ations” on Thursday), the poster session (Wednesday 9 am, Pfitzner Court), and Earpoke, the offsite concerts at the Jade Monkey (29a Twin Street) each night from 8.30 pm, whose programme lists performers (Tristan Louth-Robins, Derek Pascoe and Chris Martin, Ross Bencina, Poppi Doser, Sam Songailo; Alex Carpenter, William Menz, Jasmin Ward, Dean Roberts, Shivna Kaun; Darren Curtis, Iain Dalymple, Michael Goodfellow, Cyberbass, Rene Wooller; Chris Williams, Hidden Village, Supaphatass and VJ Sustenance, klipp av, DJ Trip) but no works. The four workshops were held on Friday 14 July.
