ACMC 2009

IMPROVISE

Queensland University of Technology, Brisbane, Australia, 2–4 July 2009

Proceedings

Improvise: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2009, edited by Andrew Sorensen. Australasian Computer Music Association, July 2009. ISSN 1448-7780. Each refereed paper, artist talk and poster below has its own page with a citation, and so does each creative work.

Complete proceedings (PDF)

The volume has no concert programme. The live performances and recorded works below are listed from the conference’s submission system, as archived in 2009, which gives each work’s session but no program notes. The evening concerts were held at The Block, QUT Kelvin Grove, on 2 and 3 July. The system also lists works by Christian Haines (evening concert, 3 July, as “[SOMETHING TO GO HEAR]”), Wendy Suiter (recorded works, 2 July) and Stephen Stanfield (recorded works, 4 July) without usable titles. The conference also had a public lecture by Paul Doornbusch, “A Brief History of Computer Music”, and workshops on AudioMulch 2.0 (Ross Bencina), Nodal (Peter McIlwain) and Impromptu (Andrew Sorensen).
