Proceedings

Interactive: Australasian Computer Music Conference 2012 Conference Proceedings, edited by Matt Hitchcock and Jodie Taylor. Australasian Computer Music Association, July 2012. ISSN 1448-7780. Each keynote, paper, artist talk, demonstration, poster, workshop and creative work below has its own page with a citation.

Complete proceedings (PDF) · Keynote abstracts (PDF) · Performances and installations: program notes and biographies (PDF) · Workshops (PDF)

The creative works are listed from the volume’s “Performances & Installations” section, with concert, performers and format from its events programme (pp. 169–174). Three works by Kaija Saariaho performed at the conference are included as the volume prints them. The foyer “selection of works” has no entry.

Review process and committees

The paper refereeing process is conducted according to the specifications of the Australian Government for the collection of Higher Education research data, and fully refereed papers therefore meet all Australian Government requirements for fully refereed research papers.
