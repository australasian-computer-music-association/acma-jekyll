ACMC 2004

Ghost in the Machine: Performance Practice in Electronic Music

Victoria University of Wellington, New Zealand, 1–3 July 2004

Proceedings

Ghost in the Machine: Performance Practice in Electronic Music – Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2004. Australasian Computer Music Association, July 2004. ISSN 1448-7780. Each keynote, paper, artist talk and studio report below has its own page with a citation, and so does each work in the concert schedule.

Complete proceedings (PDF) · Papers and concerts programme (PDF)

The concert works are listed from the schedule printed in the proceedings, which has no program notes. The schedule also lists the Douglas Lilburn complete electronic works CD launch (Concert 2), a concert by UNTREF’s Fronteras del Silencio from Argentina (Concert 3), and untitled live sets in Concert 8 (While You Were Sleeping) by Ryan Cockburn (turntables), Emile De La Rey (live electronics), Signer (guitar and live electronics) and the VJs of the WYWS Collective (live video).
