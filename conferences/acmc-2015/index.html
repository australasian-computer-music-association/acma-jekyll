ACMC 2015

MAKE!

University of Technology Sydney, 18–21 November 2015

Conference website (archived copy)

Proceedings

ACMC2015 – MAKE! Proceedings of the Annual Conference of the Australasian Computer Music Association, edited by Ian Stevenson. Australasian Computer Music Association, November 2015. ISSN 1448-7780. Each paper, abstract, artist talk and studio report below has its own page with a citation, and so does each work in the concerts.

Complete proceedings (PDF) · Abstracts (PDF)

The volume has no concert programme. The concert works below are listed from the conference website’s day pages (Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday), which give program notes and biographies for the evening concerts. The Saturday works were played in Physical Meets Virtual, a live ABC Classic FM broadcast for Australian Music Month from Studio 227; its line-up is from the Saturday page, which also lists Nigel Helyer’s Biologging_Retrofit (entered under Concert 2). The site’s schedule page lists Luke Harrald’s The Hypnotist (live performer and interactive electronics) in the broadcast instead, and gives Atherton and Drummond’s work no title. The conference also had a Data Arena session (Andrew Bluff and Ben Simons), a listening walk (Sophea Lerner), lunchtime demonstrations in the Interactivation Studio (Rodney Berry, Tangible Bytebeats; Bert Bongers; Damian Castaldi, Play Ongaku), a NIME 2016 presentation (Andrew Brown) and Cat Hope’s keynote, none of which are printed in the volume.

Review process and committees

The peer review process applied to full papers published in these proceedings involved impartial and independent assessment and review of the papers before publication, conducted by independent, qualified experts as required by section 6.5 of the 2015 Higher Education Research Data Collection Specifications of the Australian Government Department of Education and Training.
