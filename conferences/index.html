The Australasian Computer Music Conference (ACMC)

Since 1993, the annual Australasian Computer Music Conference (ACMC) has been held in Australia or New Zealand. These conferences include research papers, artist talks, studio reports, concerts, workshops, and poster sessions, and proceedings have generally been published in full.

The 2026 Australasian Computer Music Conference (ACMC2026), Resistance Music, will take place on 7-8 December 2026 at University of Queensland, Brisbane, Australia. See this link for submission and attendance information.

  • We are always searching for potential hosts for ACMC. We have information for hosting on this page.
  • All of our conferences are subject to our Code of Conduct
  • Proceedings and further information from conferences are available via the links below. Papers, abstracts and creative works are also listed in the proceedings index and all creative works.
