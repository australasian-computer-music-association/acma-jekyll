---
title: "Privacy"
layout: page
description: "How the Australasian Computer Music Association collects, uses and stores members' personal information."
---

ACMA collects your name, email address and ACMC registration details so we can confirm your membership and add you to our register of members. We use your email to send you association notices, such as notice of the AGM, and, if you ask us to, news about future conferences.

Your details are held by the ACMA secretary and committee. Under the [ACMA constitution]({% link assets/minutes/ACMA_constitution.pdf %}) (clause 5), members may inspect the register of members, which lists each member's name, contact address and date of joining. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else, and we only use it for running the association.

Membership responses are collected with Google Forms and stored by the committee. We keep your details while you are a member and remove them after your membership lapses.

To see, correct or remove your details, or to resign your membership, email [info@computermusic.org.au](mailto:info@computermusic.org.au).
