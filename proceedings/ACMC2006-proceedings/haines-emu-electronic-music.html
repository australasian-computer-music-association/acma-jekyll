Studio report

EMU, Electronic Music Unit, University of Adelaide

Christian Haines and Stephen Whittington

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2006, Adelaide, Elder Conservatorium of Music. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Abstract

2006 saw the introduction of new courses that expand the range of offerings available to undergraduate students. The aim is both to provide greater depth in certain areas, as well to give wider breadth of coverage, particularly in awareness of current trends and issues affecting music technology. A student forum was introduced that gives opportunities for the discussion of issues (one issue discussed was “What is Music Technology?”). Further, the forum has allowed for presentations to the entire student body by visiting artists and guest speakers (so far this year speakers have included Robin Minard, Warren Burt and Gordon Monro).
