Creative work · Refereed

I Will Be Your Co-Pilot

Dylan Davis

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, pp. 81–82. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

This project examines the relationship between future, science, technology, and music through an ambient drone composition. The piece features a looping structure that creates a continuous auditory experience reflecting these themes. The ambient drone acts as a metaphor for the ongoing progression of time and innovation, providing a soundscape that encourages thoughtful consideration of human development and future possibilities. The aim of this work is to offer a reflective auditory environment that prompts contemplation about the direction of human progress and potential futures. The drone’s consistent presence symbolizes the steady advancement of technology, while the ambient layers depict the evolving nature of technological change. The accompanying video features formless, organic shapes designed to align with the tonal qualities of the drone. These abstract visuals enhance the overall experience by creating a cohesive multisensory engagement that appeals to both hearing and sight. The fluid, shape-shifting visuals represent the dynamic and unpredictable aspects of technological evolution, supporting the conceptual framework of the composition. Together, the sound and visuals provide insight into the implications of technological and scientific advancements. This project serves as an inquiry into the interaction between human creativity and technological progress, inviting reflection on the future of societal evolution and the effects of contemporary innovations on future generations.
