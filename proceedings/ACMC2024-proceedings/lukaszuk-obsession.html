Creative work · Refereed

Obsession

Michael Lukaszuk

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, p. 15. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

In addition to acting as a reflection on my own experiences with the instrument, this piece explores on the guitar as a characteristically electroacoustic instrument – one that moves naturally between technologically-mediated found sounds and virtuosic playing techniques that have shaped different genre trajectories. It includes recordings of myself playing the guitar, along with a substantial amount of sound samples that use different software techniques to model the sounds of the instrument. These range from earlier instrument synthesis techniques such as FM and cross synthesis, in which the software can produce various digital artifacts, to AI based timbre applications. The entire composition is a structured around three sections that involve a gradual progression from actual guitar recordings that are edited to form abstract textures to an ending in which the foreground material consists of synthesized guitar samples that produce more discernably guitaristic sounds–conveying a clearer sense of rhythm, melody, and harmony despite being realized using pre-programmed patterns in an algorithmic composition software.
