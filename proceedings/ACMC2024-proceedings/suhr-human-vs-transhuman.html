Creative work · Peer reviewed (abstract)

Human vs. Transhuman Act 1 & Act 2

Cecilia Suhr

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, pp. 74–75. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

Human vs. Transhuman: Act one critically examines the transhumanism movement, which involves the exploration of human development through genome editing, technological augmentation, AI, and so on. This first introduction, which consists of several acts, investigates the process of humans being modified with nanochip technology. In doing so, live voice improvisation and speech are combined with live electronics to explore the ethical quandaries around this concept. This work comprises some images created using A.I. Overall, this interactive audio-visual experimental video explores what it means to be human in the age of the fourth revolution. Act 2 of Human vs. Transhuman critically examines the transhumanism movement, which advocates for human evolution through technological augmentation, artificial intelligence, and other means. As the live human voice and bamboo flute blend in and interact with live electronics, it expresses the perplexity and confusion of a human doubting his or her identity; in doing so, it raises the question of what it means to be human as the line between man and machine blurs.
