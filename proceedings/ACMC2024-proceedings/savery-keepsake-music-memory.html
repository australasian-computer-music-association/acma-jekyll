Creative work · Refereed

Keepsake: Music, Memory and Machine

Anna Savery

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, pp. 32–33. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

Keepsake: Music, Memory, and Machine is an audiovisual composition for solo augmented violin and interactive visuals that explores human-agent interaction within a live performance. The piece features home footage of my children dancing, processed in real-time using Processing software. As the violinist, my gestures and audio, processed through Max/MSP, directly influence the visuals. Wekinator serves as a bridge between these software platforms, enabling interaction and data exchange. The composition uses probability-based algorithms to shape its structure, introducing an element of surprise that keeps me engaged as a performer and fosters a collaborative interaction with the virtual agent. The video processing adapts to data captured over time, giving the visuals a sense of agency and creating an improvisational feel throughout the performance. Nostalgic elements are woven into the work through the use of music box samples and visualizations of old family photos. These images are constructed using particle systems that flow across the screen and gradually disappear, contrasting digital visuals with personal memories. Keepsake invites the audience to reflect on human interaction and memory in a digital performance context. This piece is a work-in-progress, which the uploaded video reflects.
