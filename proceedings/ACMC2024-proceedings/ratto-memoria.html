MEMORIA

Diego Ratto

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, p. 63. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

A tempestuous journey through time, into the profound recesses of memory. Hidden and distant realms of moments and snapshot recollections, a personal odyssey guided by the echoes of childhood sounds.

Printed in the proceedings, p. 63 (PDF)

Presented
