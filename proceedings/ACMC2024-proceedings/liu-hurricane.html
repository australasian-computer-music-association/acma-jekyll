Creative work · Refereed

Hurricane

Jiayi Liu

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, pp. 42–43. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

The Hurricane was inspired by a documentary film that describes the formation process and state of hurricanes from a meteorological point of view, and also shows the destructive power of hurricanes and their ravages on the natural ecology through the perspective of survivors. I have divided this piece into five parts: 1. 00：00-2:00 nature’s foreshadowing of the hurricane and its formation, 2. 2:00-7:30 the first movement of the hurricane, 3. 7:30-9:30 the brief clam between the two movements, 4. 9:30-13:30 the second movement of the hurricane, 5. 13:30-15:00 the aftermath of the hurricane. Survivors mentioned that the sound of the hurricane they heard in the hallway resembled the timbre of a clarinet, so the sound source for the entire work was primarily from the bass clarinet and the Chinese zither, with 99 per cent of the sound coming from the bass clarinet. Each part incorporates the sound of the hurricane in its various stages. The first part shows nature’s foreshadowing of the hurricane’s arrival, and I have included simulated animal sounds, such as whales and birdsong, to show the sensitivity of animals to natural changes; the second part is the first movement of the hurricane, which comes with ferocity despite the foreshadowing, and the different timbres of the wind intertwine in the eye of the wind; the third part depicts the tragic situation of the hurricane after its first passage, such as the sounds of a power station being destroyed, electric current being shorted, and a thunderbolt and earthquake, and hints at the arrival of a second outburst; Part IV is the second movement of the hurricane, which is unexpected and brings even more destruction; and Part V shows the devastation after the hurricane, containing faint distress signals, the sound of helicopters flying in the distance, and the sound of reconstruction after rescue. The Hurricane makes extensive use of the bass clarinet’s various performance techniques, such as High Harmonics，Pista，Air Sound，Low Ctremolos，Tremoli… combined with the zither’s sweeping and strumming techniques, and transforms these sounds into the hurricane’s process, mainly through the offset and filtering functions of the CR8 sampler and the use of audio transposition, downspeeding, shifting, and rewinding, as well as effects such as the note repeater and modular. various sounds. I tried to reproduce the whole process of the hurricane as described in the documentary through sound, so that the listener can experience the power of the hurricane as if he/ she were watching a film through his/her ears.
