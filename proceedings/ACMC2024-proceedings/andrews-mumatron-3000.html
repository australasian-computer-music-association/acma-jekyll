Creative work · Refereed

Mumatron 3000

Rhonda Andrews

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, p. 59. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

“Mumatron 3000” is a Daft Punk-inspired track that blends emotional depth with innovative production. Rooted in Teka’s personal journey from Kiribati to Australia and beyond, the composition fuses traditional influences with modern techniques. The song, inspired by Teka’s Mother’s Day letter to his mother, Emma, reflects both his cultural heritage and the multifaceted nature of contemporary motherhood. Featuring trumpet, and a futuristic sound design with MXR Talkbox and Moog Voyager, the track was crafted using Ableton Live and various advanced audio tools. “Mumatron 3000” showcases a unique combination of personal storytelling, cultural pride, and cuttingedge music production.
