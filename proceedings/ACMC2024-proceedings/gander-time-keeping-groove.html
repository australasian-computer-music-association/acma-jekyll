Time Keeping the Groove: Pivot Frames with Andy Gander

Andy Gander

In ACMC 2024 Proceedings, The Australian Institute of Music, Sydney and Melbourne, Australia, p. 71. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Abstract

I propose a thirty-minute lecture/recital to show how I developed the Future Research section of my PhD thesis into a book of timekeeping beats, phrases and groove concepts based on quintuplet and septuplet subdivisions. The book establishes a rhythmic framework (and “personal pedagogy”) for recalibrating 8th and 16th-note vernacular archetypes of Funk/R&B drumming to form tuplet- based analogs of those patterns. Specifically, this involves staggering the subdivisions of traditional “straight” 8th and 16-note grooves based on a system of “pivots frames”, while maintaining conventional quarter-note pulse and backbeat structures. The presentation will outline the basics of this method using notation (as below), sound files and live demonstrations. I would need a screen for displaying Powerpoint slides, an audio system for playing MP3 files, a microphone for speaking and a basic drum-kit.
