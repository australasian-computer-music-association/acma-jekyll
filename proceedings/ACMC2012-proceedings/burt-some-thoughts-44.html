Artist talk

Some Thoughts on 44 Years of Interactive Music Making

Warren Burt

In Interactive: Australasian Computer Music Conference 2012 Conference Proceedings, Brisbane, Queensland Conservatorium, Griffith University, pp. 104–109. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Abstract

In 1968, as an undergraduate student at Albany, my teacher Joel Chadabe introduced us to a new concept he’d been working on: interactive composing. Not without some questioning (how was this different from collective improvising? Or mowing the lawn?), I accepted this concept, and have been making interactive works in one form or another ever since. Today, I see the concept of interactivity as having pervaded almost every aspect of our society. In this paper, I will look at a number of pieces I’ve composed using this idea.
