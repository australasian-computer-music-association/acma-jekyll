Creative work

Skidibidou

Toby Gifford and DJ Teh

In Interactive: Australasian Computer Music Conference 2012 Conference Proceedings, Brisbane, Queensland Conservatorium, Griffith University. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

Skibidibow is every crazy cool party you’ve rocked, rolled into one song and wrapped in disco balls. Comprised of the dynamic disco duo Dr Offig and DJ Teh, Skibidibow are masters of mashtiche, blending classic party songs, dirty dubstep, and live audio manipulation. For this performance Skibidibow will throw caution to the wind and embark on a full-scale assault of live audio destruction.

Printed in the proceedings (PDF)

Performers
Dr Offig (Toby Gifford) and DJ Teh
Presented
Outside venues, Thursday 12 and Friday 13 July, 12 July 2012

About the artists

Dr Offig (Toby Gifford) is a music technologist, DJ and acoustic musician. Recent (2011) performances have included a DJ residency at the Brisbane Festival, live electroacoustic improvisation at the ElectroFringe festival, and hybrid acoustic/electronic residency at the GoMA Cinemateque performing music to accompany silent Hitchcock films.

Dr Offig’s interactive music software, the Jambot, has received national media attention, appearing on the ABC New Inventors program.
