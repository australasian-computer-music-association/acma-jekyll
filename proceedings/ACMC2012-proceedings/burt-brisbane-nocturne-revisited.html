Creative work

Brisbane Nocturne Revisited

Warren Burt

In Interactive: Australasian Computer Music Conference 2012 Conference Proceedings, Brisbane, Queensland Conservatorium, Griffith University. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

“In 2000, I wrote piece for the ACMC, which that year was held in Brisbane. It was a microtonal, algorithmic, real- time improvisational computer piece. The control part of the program was written using John Dunn’s SoftStep. The synthesis was done with Martin Fay’s Vaz Modular. For the conference, I also wrote a paper, describing in detail the processes used in the piece. For this 2012 conference, I am reconstructing the piece, but not referring to the original SoftStep patch or the recording of the original piece. Instead, I am reading the paper and reconstructing the piece from my descriptions, using the original (now obsolete) SoftStep. After the conference, I will listen to the original, and post both versions on my website, seeing if, after a lapse of 12 years, and based on my descriptions in the original paper, I came close to reconstructing the original.” (WB).

About the artist

Notes from the composer’s website: “I’m a composer, performer, video artist, sound poet, writer, instrument maker (both in hardware and software, electronic and acoustic) and a few other things. Currently, I’m living in Daylesford, Victoria, Australia, and teaching at Box Hill Institute, Melbourne, and Bendigo Regional Institute of TAFE, Bendigo. This website has an introduction to me and my work. On this page, I’m including a kind of official summary I put in grant applications, job searches, etc. It gives some idea of what I’ve been up to….

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS 1984 – present: Born 1949 Baltimore, Md. USA; lived in Waterford NY, 1950-1971; San Diego CA 1971-75; Melbourne, Australia 1975- 2004; Wollongong 2004-present. Education; BA 1971 State U. of NY at Albany; MA 1975 University of California, San Diego, PhD 2007 University of Wollongong
