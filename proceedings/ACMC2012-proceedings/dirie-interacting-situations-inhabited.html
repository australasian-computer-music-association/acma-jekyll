Artist talk

Interacting with Situations, Inhabited Spaces, and Travelogues: A Case of a Wedding in the Xochimilco Park

Gerardo Dirié

In Interactive: Australasian Computer Music Conference 2012 Conference Proceedings, Brisbane, Queensland Conservatorium, Griffith University, p. 117. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Abstract

In this Artist Talk, composer Gerardo Dirié refers to an international collaboration with a Mexican virtuoso harp duo, ambience sounds from a wedding in the lake park of Xochimilco, and the expansion of artistic interactions with a city, its inhabitants, and their transit through the environment.

Gerardo Dirié, harpists Mercedes Gómez Benet and Janet Paulus collaborate with a situationalist workshop for architecture and design students at the UNAM (Universidad Nacional Autonoma de Mexico), led by Francisco Erzo and Jhavier Lloeza.

Their multi experiential interfacing allows them to reach transformative levels of interpretation and knowledge, leading to a holistic educational experience that enhances the hybridization of selves with the environments in which the actions take place, all projected or published later in interrelated media. Website: http://creaverbu.posterous.com/ www.gerardodirie.net

The presentation will include playing a CD with the studio version of “Arpegios a Xochimilco (for prerecorded sounds and improvising harps), and the projection of visual materials (quicktime movies, ppt, pdf).
