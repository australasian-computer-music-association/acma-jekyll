Creative work

Tints of July

Haruka Hirayama

In Interactive: Australasian Computer Music Conference 2012 Conference Proceedings, Brisbane, Queensland Conservatorium, Griffith University. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

The timbres of Guitar and Flute sound best the tones of my July. Dappled sunlight, shimmering, pleasure, transience, quiet rain and a feeling of openness…… This is the first composition for flute, guitar and live electronics, and various extended techniques were explored in order to extract as much unique desirable timbres as possible. As a main compositional concern, creating organic sound relationship between musical instruments and computer was sought so that audience can be engaged. The electronic sound was created in the Max/ MSP programming environment, and has been renewed with each performance: as a primary change, three pre-recorded and modified sound clips were also included after an original performance, where all of the electronic sound was generated in real-time.

About the artist

Haruka studied composition and computer music with Cort Lippe and Takayuki Rai at Kunitachi College of Music in Tokyo. She was a winner of the Residence Prize at the 32nd International Competition of Electroacoustic Music and Sonic Art, and invited to the Institute for Electroacoustic Music in Sweden (EMS) as a composer-in-residence. Her interactive pieces for musical instruments and computers have also been performed at the International Computer Music Conferences of 2005, 2006 and 2009, and various works have been performed in many countries. Currently she lives in the UK, and has been a PhD student in Electroacoustic composition at the NOVARS Research Centre, The University of Manchester under the supervision of Dr. Ricardo Climent.
