Approximating Objective Aesthetic Assessment with Artificial Neural Network

Robert Wolf

In Interactive: Australasian Computer Music Conference 2012 Conference Proceedings, Brisbane, Queensland Conservatorium, Griffith University, pp. 78–83. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Abstract

This article discusses the possibility of using an Artificial Neural Network to provide aesthetic assessments of automatically generated counterpoints. It presents in detail several different architectures of such networks and analyses training strategies. In particular, it demonstrates how to train such a network for providing objective assessments (such as those prescribed by course materials on counterpoint) and why this approach is not entirely practical. It also observes that Artificial Neural Networks may, in fact, exhibit some form of machine subjectivity.
