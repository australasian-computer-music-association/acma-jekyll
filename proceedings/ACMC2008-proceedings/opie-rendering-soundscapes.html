Rendering Soundscapes

Timothy Opie

In ACMC08 SOUND : SPACE – Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2008, Sydney Conservatorium of Music, pp. 46–49. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Abstract

The recording of an aural soundscape can be used as a structural framework in which the composer can reconceptualize and reshape the entire space. Using an eco-structuralist process the recording is reduced to a framework that affords an entirely new sonic perception, whilst retaining a sense of the original space. This paper discusses the framework and rendering of a seaside sound event.
