In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2023, University of New England, Sydney campus, Parramatta, NSW, pp. 43–44. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

Modular synthesisers intertwine with the enchanting sounds of acoustic sounds and instruments. #FoxAndDrum #ModularSynthesis #KochiBells #LiveStudioEP #SonicJourney #MusicalExploration
