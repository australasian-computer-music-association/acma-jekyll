Creative work

Electronica Bass

Mansell Laidler

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2023, University of New England, Sydney campus, Parramatta, NSW, p. 43. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

Welcome to Electronica Bass, where the electric bass takes centre stage as a powerful interface for the creation of electronic music. Throughout this performance, the artist harnesses the boundless potential of the electric bass, moulding its raw sound through a carefully curated collection of effects and digital amplifier models. Using the versatile software looping platform Ableton, the processed bass parts are looped, building intricate layers of sonic richness that defy convention.

Printed in the proceedings, p. 43 (PDF)

Format
one channel
Duration

Presented
Lanes Concert — Techalia: The Muse of Technology & Invention, Car Park behind 28 George St Parramatta (next to Eat St Car Park), part of the Parramatta Lanes Festival, 11 October 2023

About the artist

Mansell Laidler is a seasoned bass player with a 25-year career in the music industry, including a decade as a member of the Australian Army Band. In 2020, during the pandemic, he discovered the world of Live Looping and software looping, combining the electric bass with effects and software-based loopers. He was accepted into UNE’s Master of Philosophy program in May 2021, undertaking a research project titled “Electronica Bass: The Electric Bass as an Interface for the creation of Electronic Music.”
