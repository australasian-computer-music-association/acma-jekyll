Creative work

String Theory

Mark Oliveiro

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2023, University of New England, Sydney campus, Parramatta, NSW, p. 34. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

String Theory is an interactive concert work that intertwines the resonant tones of the Paetzold contrabass recorder, live electronic music featuring sub-bass frequencies, and immersive video projections inspired by Galileo Galilei’s celestial research. Designed using MaxMSP, this experience takes the audience on a journey through the harmonious realms of both music and astronomy. Through MaxMSP, the recorder’s melodies and textures blend seamlessly with live electronic manipulations, blurring the boundaries between the earthly and the ethereal. (To be performed by Alana Blackburn.)

Dr Mark Oliveiro is a graduate and distinguished alumnus of the Sydney Conservatorium, the University of North Texas and the School of Music of Indiana University (see Paper Session 2B).
