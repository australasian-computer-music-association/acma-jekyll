Creative work

125±10Hz x4

Fern Bravo

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2023, University of New England, Sydney campus, Parramatta, NSW, p. 39. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

This piece explores auditory phenomena through multiple sine tones clashing with each other across the frequency spectrum and stereo field. These are then sent through tonal and phase-shifting effects and returned to sum together.

Printed in the proceedings, p. 39 (PDF)

Format
2/4 channels
Duration
10:49
Presented
Spatial Audio 2 — Astraline: The Muse of Astronomy, Space and Acoustics, UNE, 11 October 2023

About the artist

fern is a multidisciplinary sound artist/producer based in Tāmaki Makaurau. Their work explores sound production and the external experience of tactile composition and tone shaping, in the melding of signals interacting through hardware and software.
