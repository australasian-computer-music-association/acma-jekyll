Creative work · Refereed

A.002

Andrew R. Brown

In ACMC 2020: Proceedings of the 2020 Australasian Computer Music Conference, Online (Australian National University, Canberra). Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

This work involves performer meta-control of three autonomous musical agents that employ the Gendynish algorithm (Brown 2019). In the spirit of post-digital aesthetics, the work exploits the materiality of Arduino-class microprocessors to produce sounds similar to Iannis Xenakis’ generative dynamic synthesis (Xenakis 1992). The work draws inspiration from Xenakis’s innovative use of probabilistic processes to explore the nature of digital sound making and the unfolding of compositional events. The work adds to this approach, real-time, parallel, semi-autonomous, evolutionary processes that shape the sound world over time and are guided by the musician during performance or rendering.

References

Brown, Andrew R. 2019. “Gendynish: Stochastic Synthesis on the Arduino.” In Proceedings of the International Symposium on Electronic Art, 638–39. Gwangju, Korea: ISEA International

About the artist

Andrew R. Brown is an educator, researcher, musician, author and programmer. He holds a Ph.D. in music and is Professor of Digital Arts at Griffith University in Brisbane, Australia. His academic expertise is in technologies that support creativity and learning, the creation of computational music and art, and the philosophy of technology. Andrew’s creative activities focus on real time audio-visual works using generative processes and live coding performance. He has performed live interactive music in many parts of the world and his digital art works have been shown in galleries across Australia, USA and China. He is the author of Music Technology and Education: Amplifying Musicality, co-author of Making Music with Computers: Creative Programming in Python and Scratch Music Projects, and editor of Sound Musicianship: Understanding the Crafts of Music. For more information visit http://andrewrbrown.net.au
