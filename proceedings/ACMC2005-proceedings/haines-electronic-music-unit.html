Studio report

Electronic Music Unit (EMU) – Studio Report

Christian Haines

In ACMC05 Generate and Test: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2005, Brisbane, Queensland University of Technology, p. 170. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Abstract

The Electronic Music Unit (EMU) came into existence in the year 2001 within the organisation of a new music education institution, the Elder School of Music (ESM). The ESM is the product of a merger between the two primary music education institutions in South Australia - the Elder Conservatorium (EC), University of Adelaide, and the Flinders Street School of Music (FSSM), Adelaide Institute of TAFE. The creation of the ESM, and consequently EMU, was a rapid undertaking of approximately 18 months. This ensured the process was challenging, albeit engaging.
