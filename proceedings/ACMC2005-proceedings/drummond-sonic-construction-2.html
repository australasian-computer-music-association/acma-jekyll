Creative work

Sonic Construction 2

Jon Drummond

In ACMC05 Generate and Test: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2005, Brisbane, Queensland University of Technology. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

In this performed work, movent detected by a firewire video camera is used to generate a sound environment based on real-time fof synthesis and spatialisation. The slow and evolving movents of coloured dye in thick and viscous sugary water is used as the gesture or movement source.

The amount of movement, Cartesian coordinates and colour are used as time changing parameters used to control the fof synthesis and also determine the spatialisation characteristics. The performance begins with silence and an empty glass of water. As the first drop of dye is dropped into the water, the first fof sound event is generated, determined by the characteristics of the drop (speed, colour, shape) and spatialised in response to the shape unfolding of the coloured dye.

Format
water droplets, video camera and computer
Duration
15 minutes
Presented
Concert 5
