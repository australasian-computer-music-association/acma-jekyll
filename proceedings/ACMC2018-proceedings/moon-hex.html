Hex

Barry Moon

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2018, Edith Cowan University, Perth, p. 122. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 2206-5296.

Printed in the proceedings, p. 122 (PDF)

Format
six guitars with electronics
Created
2016

Program note

Hex is a piece for six guitars with electronics. Each guitarist has their own contact microphone, Raspberry Pi running Pure Data for processing, and speaker. The computers are synced on a LAN. This allows for the players to create spatial arrangements in the performance space. It also creates a sense of “chamber music” that is generally lacking in works for instrumental performance with computer processing.

Keywords: Pd, Pi Guitars, Computers, United States
