Creative work · Refereed

Constellations

Julian Scordato

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2019, Monash University, Melbourne. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

“Constellations” is a composition for IanniX graphical sequencer and electronics. It was created between March and April 2014. The world premiere occurred on October 1, 2014 at the CCRMA (Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics) of the Stanford University, in the contest of their annual Transitions concert series. Further performances and screenings took place within numerous international festivals and exhibitions.

Based on the reading of a IanniX score constituted by 356 punctual events and two drones for controlling additive synthesis parameters and bandwidth-limited noise respectively, this work assumes a certain complexity through the interaction between elementary electronic sounds in a feedback network capable of processing them synchronically and diachronically. Thus, sonic elements no longer exist just as intrinsic and independent entities; they become instead strongly characterized by global processes that transform them as part of the network.
