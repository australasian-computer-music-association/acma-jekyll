Found Sound

Donald Richards

In Waveform 2001: Australasian Computer Music Association Conference Proceedings, University of Western Sydney, pp. 75–80. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Abstract

A review with excerpts from 3 sound features broadcast by The Listening Room, the ABC’s Radiophonic program that goes to air on Monday nights.
