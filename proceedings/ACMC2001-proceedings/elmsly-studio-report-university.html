Studio report

Studio Report: University of Auckland

John Elmsly

In Waveform 2001: Australasian Computer Music Association Conference Proceedings, University of Western Sydney, p. 110. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Abstract

Does electronic music study mean aesthetic stifling, reliance on standardised models of ‘university’ computer music? This report will place particular emphasis on the undergraduate teaching activities: details of goals and methodology of our new introductory course, Music and Technology, and the 200 and 300 level papers in electronic music which account for most of our teaching. Around forty students each year are involved at various levels of studio composition that has included music for theatre, dance, film and video, and interactive compositions. Students are a mix of music majors and others from Film Studies, Science, Fine Arts, Architecture and Engineering.
