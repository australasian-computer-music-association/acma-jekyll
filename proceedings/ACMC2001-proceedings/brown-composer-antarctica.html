Artist talk

A Composer in Antarctica

Chris Cree Brown

In Waveform 2001: Australasian Computer Music Association Conference Proceedings, University of Western Sydney, p. 100. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Abstract

Recent technology in the field of sound has enabled the composer or sonic artist to analyse, explore, dissect and modify or alter every detail of sound in a way that often seems similar to the precise and specific cellular manipulations of a genetic engineer. Sonic artists can use this new technology to control the morphological and spectral elements of sound, thus providing an expressive continuum between the real, unaltered and recognisable sound, and extended transformations which yield more abstract gestures where source recognition is diffuse or dependent on context. Such specific control over the pitch and time domains of a sonic event has allowed the sonic artist to create new tensions and hierarchies that provide wonderful and vibrant opportunities for creative expression.
