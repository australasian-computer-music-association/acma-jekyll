Creative work

Sphere Within a Sphere

Antonio D'amato

In Harmony: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2014, Melbourne, Victorian College of the Arts / Melbourne Conservatorium of Music. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

Sphere within a sphere is a short piece inspired by one of Arnaldo Pomodoro’s sculptures bearing the same name. It was moulded in 1991 and placed in the square in front of the United Nations Headquarters in New York. The piece describes a complex machine which reveals by degrees and only partially his working, by means of a gradual disclosure of its inner microstructures. Sound elements, both continuous and impulsive are elaborated, mixed and overlapped several times in order to give birth to a visionary sound landscape where a monolithic and smooth shape conceals deep stratified discontinuities like linked cogwheels.

About the artist

Antonio D’amato is intoxicated by music. He graduated at conservatory in Piano, Harpsichord, Music for multimedia, Instrumental music teaching and Electronic music. He also studied composition for eight years, bassoon for three years, baroque organ and audio engineering. In 2010 he was Ondes Martenot student in Strasbourg and Paris. At university he was student in Media and Communication. At the moment his main interest is joining traditional composition procedures and the wide opportunities of computer-based music. Some of his instrumental works are published by Forton Music, U.K. His first electronic composition was selected for a performance during the ICMC 2012 Conference. Other works were performed in Australia, Brazil, Italy, Taiwan and USA.
