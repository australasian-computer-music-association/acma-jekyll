Field Effect: Movement, Sound and Semblance in Audio/Visual Composition

Mark Pedersen

In Harmony: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2014, Melbourne, Victorian College of the Arts / Melbourne Conservatorium of Music, pp. 79–82. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Abstract

This article takes Brian Massumi’s examination of semblance and event as the starting point for building an audio/visual composition framework. Field Effect, a specific work arising from this investigation, is discussed and reflections on the composition process are offered. In particular, the problem of mapping from movement to sound in interactive works is considered, and strategies for examining the mapping process through content reduction and the use of non-local linkages are presented.
