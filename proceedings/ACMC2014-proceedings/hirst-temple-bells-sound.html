Creative work

Temple Bells Sound a Welcome

David Hirst

In Harmony: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2014, Melbourne, Victorian College of the Arts / Melbourne Conservatorium of Music. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

Temple bells are manipulated to sound a welcome for visitors to Melbourne in 2014. David Hirst has composed and researched electroacoustic music for over 30 years, and is currently Honorary Principal Fellow at the School of Contemporary Music, University of Melbourne.
