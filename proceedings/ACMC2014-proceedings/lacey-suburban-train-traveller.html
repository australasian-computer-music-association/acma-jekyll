Creative work

A Suburban Train Traveller

Jordan Lacey

In Harmony: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2014, Melbourne, Victorian College of the Arts / Melbourne Conservatorium of Music. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

Jordan Lacey is a Melbourne-based creative practitioner, who is presently completing a PhD in Urban Sound Design at RMIT University. Recently, when traveling on the suburban trains of Melbourne I met an unusual young man who spends his days riding the trains collecting sound recordings of train and station announcements with his mp3 recorder. He told me he had collected nearly every announcement, and offered to send me a copy. I’ve always wanted to do something with these recordings for which 60*60 seemed the perfect opportunity.
