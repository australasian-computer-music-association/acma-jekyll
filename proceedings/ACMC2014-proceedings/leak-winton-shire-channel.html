Creative work

Winton Shire Channel 1

Graeme Leak

In Harmony: Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2014, Melbourne, Victorian College of the Arts / Melbourne Conservatorium of Music. Australasian Computer Music Association, ISSN 1448-7780.

Program note

Council radio field recordings mixed with string cans, tube resonance recordings and a rock plop. Graeme Leak is a composer, performer and musical director who likes using found and everyday objects in the studio.

Printed in the proceedings (PDF)

Presented
