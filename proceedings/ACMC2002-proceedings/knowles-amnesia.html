Creative work

Amnesia

Julian Knowles

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

The video materials for Amnesia were all captured in a one hour period from 6.30-7.30pm whilst on the foreshores of Sydney Harbour. This idea of limiting myself to materials captured in a specific timeframe is an idea I have worked with in the piece ‘one_day’ (2000). The main visual element is the surface of the water and refracted light through the dusk period into night. The audio materials are a combination of materials recorded in oil and water tanks on location at Casula Powerhouse (an old coal fired power station), ‘ghetto blaster feedback’ in the aforementioned acoustic spaces and digitally reprocessed guitar. Materials collected from an urbanised traditional site, Bennelong Point, are framed to avoid the urban elements (probing for history), which becomes increasingly difficult as night falls and the urban space becomes part of the water surface via reflected light. This sets up a potent irony, as one becomes seduced by the modulating light underneath the foreboding ‘scape’ of the Harbour Bridge. The soundtrack of the power station underpins this journey. This piece was originally presented as part of the Placescapes exhibition at Goya Galleries, Melbourne.

About the artist

Julian Knowles is a composer/performer working with computers, sound synthesis, field recording and digital audio processing. His work spans studio and live performance, composition, and sound design for film, exploring the inherent (im)possibilities of various audio manoeuvres and uncovers the musicality of unlikely sound sources. He synthesises elements from an eclectic range of sonic practices such as acousmatic music, film sound, location recording, pop music production techniques, surround sound and audio dsp. As a solo performer, Julian has toured the UK, USA, Latin America and Korea to critical acclaim and is also a member of the group Social Interiors (Extreme Records). He is currently a Senior Lecturer and Electronic Arts/Music Technology Co-ordinator at the University of Western Sydney.
