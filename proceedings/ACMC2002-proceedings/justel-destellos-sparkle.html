Creative work

Destellos (Sparkle)

Elsa Justel

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

As in a reverie, the objects separate from their sense to become poetry.

The idea of the project is to give life to the sparkle in different materials. Metal, glasses, ice, will travel in time and space by means of computer animation. There is also a play with sensations of fragility and transparency. The music plays the same notions by using recorded sounds of the same materials. In fact, the discourse is lead by the music which guides the time development and underlines the sense of colour.

About the artist

(Argentine-1944) Living in France since 1988. She studied composition and electroacoustics in Buenos Aires, then she had her Doctor degree in Computer music at the University of Paris. She taught Avant-garde music at the Conservatory of Mar del Plata, since 1980.

She has received composition awards such as: Prix Ars Electronica, Linz, Austria (1992, by “Fy-Mor” computer music), Stipendienpreis of Darmstadt, Germany (1990), International electroacoustic competition of Bourges, France (1989, by “Ichihualasto”, electroacoustic), Tribuna de Música electroacústica de Argentina (1996, by “Fy Mor” for tape) and Tribuna Nacional de compositores of Argentina (1987, by “Abismos” for voice and live electronics).

Major international festivals which have included performances of her works are the ICMC, in Montreal (Canada-91), San José, California (United States-92), The Banff Centre (Canada-95), Hong Kong (96), Thessaloniki (Creece-97), Festival Synthese of Bourges (France-90-95-96), Ferienkurse für neue musik, Darmstadt (Germany-90-92), Elektronischer Frühling, Vienna (Austria-94), New Music Festival (Denmark-92), ENSEMS Festival of contemporary music (Valencia, Spain-96), XIII Settimana per i beni musicali, Bologna (Italy-92), Festival of electroacoustic music of Bogotà (Colombia), as well as concerts and broadcasts in United States, Europe, Canada, Australia and latinamerican countries.

Between the commissions and grants she was asked the composition of electroacoustic pieces in France (“Feuillage de Silence” for flute, oboe and electronics-GRM, Paris / “Veras llorar la Biblia” for tape-GES, Vierzon / “Le jardin de nuages” for tape-GMEB, Bourges / “ALL+” for double bass and electronics-Mixture, Paris: “Alba Su”, IMEB, Bourges / “Mâts” INA-GRM, Paris / “El tercer fuego” Radio France, Paris), in Austria (“Sikxo” for saxophone and tape - Elektronik Musik studio of the Hochschule of Viena), in Belgium (“La ventana deshabitada” for harpsichord and tape - IPEM, Gent / “Pieza en forma de té” electroacoustic, Musiques et Recherches, Ohain), and in Spain (“Latido Estival” for voice and tape -LIEM, Madrid / “La ilusion sensible” audiovisual -Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona / “Midi de Sable” Studio Phonos, Barcelone), in Germany (“Au Loin…bleu” for tape - Exposition “Multilinear” Hannover).

Works recorded by: J&W-Canada, CD931 (“La ventana deshabitada” for harpsichord and tape); ICMC ‘96, Hong Kong (“Haricots et petits bâtons” for tape); Diffusion i Media, Canada, IMED 9837 (“Chi-pa-boo” for tape); 00Discs, USA, 0045 (“Fy Mor” for tape); Acousmatica, France, CD1200 (“Mâts” for tape); Organised Sound Vol. 3, Cambridge University Press, (“Au loin… bleu” for tape), Computer Music Journal, Vol.25-N°4,2001 (“Alba Sud” for tape)
