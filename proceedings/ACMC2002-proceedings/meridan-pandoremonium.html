Creative work

Pandoremonium

Lissa Meridan

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

pandoremonium started life as a dance track by San Francisco based duo, Waterjuice. The resulting remix explores deconstruction and reconstruction of the original material, a dissection of the sonic qualities intrinsic in the beat, an implosion and explosion of the space between dance music and more abstract sound material. The mix was performed and recorded in real time at VaporVent studios, San Francisco. The original track, Pandora’s Box, was released by Eyefunk Records, and can be found on the album “Hydroponics”.

About the artist

Lissa Meridan (b. 1972) is Director of the Electroacoustic Music Studios at Victoria University of Wellington, where she also teaches instrumental composition, orchestration, counterpoint and acoustics. She is a committee member and webmaster for the Composers Assoc. of NZ and Vice President of the Australasian Computer Music Association. Her works are regularly broadcast on ConcertFM, National Radio and have been performed at concerts and in galleries and festivals in NZ, Australia, USA, Korea, Czechoslovakia, and Bulgaria. She has composed for a wide variety of media, including orchestra, chamber ensembles and electroacoustics. She regularly organises concerts of electroacoustic music and performs live electronic music, both as a sound artist and turntablist (a.k.a. fierce angel). Recent recordings are available on New Zealand Sonic Arts Vol 2, Sonic 2000: New Zealand Electronic Music, and the Atoll release Fanfares for the New Millienium.
