Creative work

Birdspeaker Piece #3

Peter McIlwain

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

The Birdspeaker Pieces are a series of works that are generated by the SNet software. This software employs a behavioural-based model of biological neural nets. It is designed to generate spatio-temporal patterns by allocating audio events to 16 independent audio channels (this can be reduced to 8 or 4 channels if necessary). The software was developed by Peter Mcilwain who is Lecturer in Composition at the School of Music – Conservatorium at Monash University.

About the artist

Peter is a composer specialising in computer and electro acoustic music but has a wide range of musical and artistic interests. These include writing works for traditional ensembles, film and music theatre as well as computer music and installation art. Recent projects have included the development of software which generates and distributes music in multi-speaker sound systems using a connectionist nodal network approach as well as the development of a flocking algorithm which generates melodies in response to movements by a performer using an ultrasonic sensor. He is Lecturer in Composition and Music Technology in the School of Music - Conservatorium at Monash University where he is also a member of Dome the contemporary gamelan ensemble based at the University.
