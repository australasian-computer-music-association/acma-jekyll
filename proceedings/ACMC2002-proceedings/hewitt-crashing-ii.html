Creative work

Crashing II

Donna Hewitt and Nell Andrew

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

Crashing was originally from a larger dance work called Beyond the Depths - a collaboration between choreographer, Nell Andrew and composer, Donna Hewitt, which was performed at the Edge Theatre in Sydney in February 2002. Our mutual interest in the ocean and its plethora of environments and moods inspired the work. The pieces explore the diverse life forms and environments that exist within the ocean, including the various layers or ‘zones’ ie the ‘Midnight’, ‘Moonlight’ and the ‘Twilight’ Zone. Crashing sought to explore some of the more extreme of ocean environments and was one of the more successful works. Crashing II is a development of the original piece, which extends and develops the original musical ideas to form a stand alone work, and has been re-arranged for multi-channel sound to enhance the immersive experience for the listener. The source sounds are drawn from a library of captured and found sound, including air and hydrophone recordings of marine environments and found objects which are subjected to digital reprocessing. These sound sources are drawn upon for live processing and mixing via AudioMulch, allowing for close communication between dancer and composer during the performance.

About the artists

Donna Hewitt is a composer/performer working with voice, environmental and computer processed sound. Much of her work involves re-processing audio via various digital processes to produce complex textures. As a PhD scholarship student in the Music Area at UWS, she is working with multichannel audio and has completed a number of studio pieces in the Dolby 5.1 surround format. Recently she has been exploring real time digital performance systems and custom interfaces in object/patcher environments. Recent performances/screenings include ‘Liquid Extrusion’ ArtHouse 2002, ‘Beyond the Depths of Silence’ The Edge Theatre 2002 ‘Peep Show’ Most Significant Bytes 2001 Concert Series, Ohio, USA; Impermanent Audio 2001, Waveform 2001, Electronic Antiphony, Melbourne 2001, Sonic Residues 02 Australian Centre for Contemporary Art, Melbourne 2000; Llewers Gallery, Fuse Exhibition 1999, The Mill on the Floss (Sydney Theatre Company/Babylon Productions) The Wharf Theatre 1999, ‘Drowning’ Watt concert 1998. Her work is released on a number of compilation CDs.
