Creative work

Fractured Delay #4

The Sonic Art Group

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

Fractured Delay #4 is from a series of pieces that feature process sound from a variety of sound sources. This piece features a performance on a piano frame that is processed by the fractured delay software. The software uses eight gated delay lines that break up (or fracture) the input sound into segments that are processed by the delay lines. The software can be used for live performance with an eight channel sound system. It can produce complex and varied textures that evolve over time.

Format
piano frame and fractured delay software, eight-channel sound system

About the artist

The Sonic Art Group is based in the School of Music – Conservatorium at Monash University. Its major role is to function as a research unit directed by Peter Mcilwain – Lecturer in Composition. In addition, it provides opportunities for composition students at the School to gain experience in experimental computer based music. Since starting at the beginning of 2001 it has; performed in a combined concert with Simulation (a group based at QUT) and at the Make It Up Club and has staged the Electronic Antiphony concert. The group’s creative work is focused on the composition of sonic textures through improvisation. Much of this work is centred around software developed by the Peter Mcilwain, in conjunction with other members of the group.
