Creative work

Canopies: Chimerical Acoustic Environments

Lawrence Harvey

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

Canopies was originally commissioned by the Southgate Arts and Leisure Precinct for their Soundscape System located in central Melbourne. This system comprises 160 loud speakers distributed along approximately 200 metres of the Yarra River promenade and controlled via an audio server.

The image of a woven sonic lattice and the speaker configuration of the Soundscape playback system suggest a covering suspended or held overhead; a canopy. The environments suspended within a canopy are chimerical; fictitious, fanciful, fantastic, fictional, illusory. Listening to the soundscape of an environment we may hear and imagine a weave of connections and transformations; the real and imagined fuse into the other.

The predominantly low frequency nature of the city soundscape, had to be taken into account while designing the sounds for Canopies. This led to the use of sounds in mid to high frequency bands that are richly textured and varied. The sound sources for the work included transformed recordings made from a set of wood-chimes, a collection of shells, a set of beads and small brass bells and cymbals. These sound sources were selected for their ability to create dense sonic complexes. Other material was created from various synthesis techniques and transformed vocal improvisations.

Canopies installation version is designed for a transitional space; a stairwell, an arcade, or the promenade of a river. The concert realisation provides a sense of transition through several differentiated environments and combines material from the original installation, and transformed versions of those sounds.

Production for the installation version took place between November 1999 and January 2000. The original set of environments – approx 10 – formed the basis of a cyclic soundscape presented every evening (6pm-11pm) between January 18 and April 21, 2000. This concert version of the work was produced in January 2001, and was premiered at CREATE, University of California, Santa Barbara 17 May, 2001.

About the artist

Lawrence Harvey - Associate Reseacher in Acoustic Ecology and Soundscape Studies, School of Architecture and Design, RMIT, and sound designer for Metraform. Recent projects include VR sound design for “Symbiosis” shown at both the Melbourne Film (2001) and Next Wave Festivals (2002). With Metraform, he is currently completing a large commission to create “Ecstasis: human presence in digital environments”, funded by the Digital Media Fund of Film Victoria (formerly Cinemedia).
