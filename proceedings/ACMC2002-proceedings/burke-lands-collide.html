Creative work

Lands Collide

Brigid Burke

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

The concept of the composition is to make the sounds and visuals grow in and out of each other with depth and constant movement with combinations of definition and confusion but as a whole to create unity.

Another means to create this unity was the transformation of the acoustic tom toms, drums, clarinet key clicks and voice articulations performed by composer and Wendy Couch, percussion into lyrically thick percussive sounds overlapping themselves to form complex rhythms. These short percussive rhythmic patterns are overlaid and textured into ten channels both live and prerecorded.

Throughout Lands Collide old maps and landscape that has been untainted by man, Javanese flutes and gongs, organic drawings also largely influenced me. The sounds and visuals are unique, like the wind, full of rich sonorities just waiting to be transformed or left alone. The aim was also to try and convey moments, portraying a simplistic, guttural, earthy yet sophisticated sound field and landscape as one can feel when in nature and experiencing non-western music. This work explores various frequency changes with these sound and visual worlds through extreme contraction, expansion, panning and pitch modification. Lands Collide can also be described as an exploration of sounds at different dynamic and room placements and been embedded into different scenes by means of subtle dynamic curves both visually and aurally.

About the artist

Brigid is a composer, clarinet soloist, visual artist, and educator. She has performed extensively in solo and chamber recitals, both nationally and internationally. Performances of her compositions include in 2002 Cybersonica International Festival of Digital Music and Sound at The ICA, London, MAXIS 2002 Festival / Symposium of Audio & Art in England, art@radio on the River Hull Hull Time Based Arts, Hull, England, Seoul International Computer Music Festival 2001, and Medi@terra Festival micromuseum International Digital Audio &Video Festival, Bulgaria, Slovenia and Frankfurt. Also solo and chamber performances at Federation Music Week Contemporary Music of Australia and the Asia Pacific, St. James the Great, Grainger Museum, Melba Conservatorium and Kaliede Theatre RMIT.

Highlights of previous years include Sonic Residues2 Festival, PATO Adelaide funded by the Australia Council Performing Arts Board, BEAT International Gamelan Festival, Experimenta, Victorian Arts Centre Live and Melbourne International Festivals.

Brigid has also received twelve art awards and exhibited through out Japan in the Japanese International Hand Printed & Shhin Kohanga competition from 1989-2002.

As a freelance performer/composer Brigid is broadcast on ABC FM including local & overseas radio and is currently working on a commission from the Australian Broadcasting Commission Listening Room to be broadcast late in 2002. Her most recent CD recordings are INTERSECT, TRI DUO, BURKE & WILMOT and Electronic Compositions 1997-2001 which have been reviewed and broadcast internationally. The Performing Arts Board of the Australia Council, ANAT, South Australian Govt., Community Arts Centers and Universities have also supported

Wendy Couch: Percussionist. Following graduation from Melbourne University and the V.C.A. Wendy was awarded a Churchill Fellowship to study in California. There she completed an M.A. in Percussion Performance at San Jose State University, studying with Anthony J. Cirone. She has presented solo recitals and concertos in Australia and the U.S.A. As a freelance musician she has performed frequently with the Melbourne Symphony, Australian Pops Orchestra, Canto Coro and the Australian Boys Choir. During the last 12 years she has played for many operas and ballets with the State Orchestra of Victoria. Theatre productions include ‘Cho Cho San’ and ‘Sabat Jesus’ (Playbox), ‘Stroke’ (Chamber Made Opera), ’Les Miserables’, ‘Phantom of the Opera’, ‘The King and I’ and ‘Theatro’ (1998 Melbourne International Festival). Wendy has a vast repertoire of Chamber Music and Contemporary Percussion works and frequently performs with clarinettist Brigid Burke. She also has extensive teaching experience at both Tertiary and Secondary level. She is currently teaching Percussion at Camberwell Grammar, Mac.Robertson Girls’ High School and Canterbury Girls’ Secondary College. She has played the organ at the Melbourne Welsh Church for the last 12 years and is an A.M.E.B. Examiner.
