Creative work

Emanations

Bruce Schneider

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Program note

This piece was constructed in real time using MIDI note and controller data on the EMU Proteus 2000 synth module. Seven notes (c1, g1, c2, g2, c3, c4, c5) were allocated to seven different patches (sounds) on the Proteus and sustain on commands inserted so these sounds would effectively not stop until faded out. Up to 16 parameters in each sound may be altered in real time by controller data. This was sent to the Proteus using an analogue to MIDI converter with 16 separate control sliders. Parameters that could be controlled included tone, presence, sound shape, imaging, attack, decay, movement between layers, oscillator rates, volume and sends to internal effects processors. The MIDI data was recorded in real time using “Cakewalk” and the performance repeated until the desired outcome was achieved. The piece you are hearing was the seventh attempt.

About the artist

Bruce Schneider is a composer/audio engineer/music arranger living in Maitland (30 minutes west of Newcastle NSW) and has been involved with electronic music since the mid 1980’s. He was also a part of the composers collective Trojan Theatre until the late 1990’s along with fellow members Greg Jenkins, Eric Wilding, Ben Sutures and Noel Burgess.
