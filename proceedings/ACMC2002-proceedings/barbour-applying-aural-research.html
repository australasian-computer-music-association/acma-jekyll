Applying Aural Research: The Aesthetics of 5.1 Surround

Jim Barbour

In Proceedings of the Australasian Computer Music Conference 2002, Melbourne, RMIT University / Victorian College of Arts, pp. 17–26. Australasian Computer Music Association.

Abstract

Multi-channel surround sound audio offers a richer perceptual experience than traditional stereo reproduction, recreating three-dimensional aural soundfields. Understanding the limitations of human auditory perception and surround sound technology may assist the exploration of acoustic space as an expressive dimension.
