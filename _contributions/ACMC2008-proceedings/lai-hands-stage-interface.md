Hands On Stage is a performance interface designed from a percussionist’s perspective. It explores the inter-relationship between the performer’s hand movements, real-time video tracking techniques, and the recording and processing of sound in an integrated configuration. This paper describes the concept, interface design, technical development, and the creative approach to the visual and sound elements. The paper also discusses the project from a personal performance perspective.
