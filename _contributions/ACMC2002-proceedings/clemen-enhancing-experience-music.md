This paper presents the possibility of applying modern gesturally-controlled interactive music technology to the aesthetic framework of “music-ritual”, with the aim of enhancing the experience for the audience and participants by more directly mapping gesture with sound production. The potential of this kind of application could be to increase the communicability of symbolic meaning through concept, sound, and gesture.
