bio: Andrew Lyons began his music career as a Saxophonist playing in Melbourne’s inner city hotels during the late 1980’s. During this period, a broad range of musical influences included experimental electronic music performed on the 3RRR radio station. Following a brief period at Latrobe University in 1989, Andrew spent two years experimenting with electronic music and studio techniques at Collingwood Tafe, and another two years studying composition at the University of New England, Northern Rivers. Upon graduating in 1995, Andrew returned to Melbourne and commenced employment in the Film and Television post-production industry as a 3D animator. He continued in this line of work in Sydney from 1996 until commencing a PhD at the Sydney Conservatorium of Music in 1998. Andrew’s research concerns the composition of virtual worlds involving the visual representation of synthesized 3D sound using 3D animation tools. Andrew is currently a Visiting Research Fellow at Southern Cross University
  in Lismore.

Heisenberg is a synthesized, abstract spatial audio-visual artwork intended for DVD performance. The design of Heisenberg draws on a body of research concerning the nature of synaesthesia and mental imagery resulting from musical stimuli. In creating Heisenberg, the Houdini 3D animation software was used to complete much of the audio composition as well as the animation. Heisenberg also made use of a unique 3D sound spatialisation system involving an interface between the Houdini 3D software and Csound. Heisenberg might be described as a digitally landscaped garden of unearthly delights.
