'Maison' is the second movement of a suite called 'C'est la Vie' which explores various aspects of life. 'Maison' takes home life, or family life, as its primary source material. Toys that take over their owner's household is an age old theme that story-tellers have used for centuries. What happens when ordinary household sounds take on a life of their own? 'Maison' uses Ross Bencina's Audiomulch program extensively (see www.audiomulch.com). Original source sounds were made by Caitlin and James Hirst.
