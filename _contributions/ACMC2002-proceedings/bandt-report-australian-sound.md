The Australian Sound Design Project is a nationwide initiative to document on the web the works of Australian sound designers who work in public space. It was intitiated by Dr Ros Bandt through an ARC large grant and is hosted by the interdisciplinary Australian Centre at the University of Melbourne where she is honorary senior fellow. The research assistants, originally Garth Paine, and now Iain Mott facilitate the project, managing the database and web site programming and design.
