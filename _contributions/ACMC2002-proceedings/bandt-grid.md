bio: Ros Bandt is an internationally acclaimed sound artist, composer, researcher and scholar. Since 1977 she has pioneered interactive sound installations, sound sculptures, created sound playgrounds, spatial music systems and some 40 sound installations worldwide. She has curated many sound performances, exhibitions and events. Her original works are recorded on New Albion Records USA, Move Records, Melbourne and EMI/ABC, and Wergo Germany. In 1990 she won the Don Banks Composers Award, being the first woman to do so. Other awards include the inaugural Benjamin Cohen peace prize in the USA and the Sound Art Australia Prize also funded by the ABC and the Goethe Institute. GRID, a sonic exploration of an industrial cylinder, the city link exhaust stack, South Melbourne. Made on Sonic Solutions, Macintosh, commissioned by the Listening Room.

Grid is the 4th movement of the larger electroacoustic work STACK. The Architectural form of the exoskeleton cylinder has been a sonic shaper, at a variety of levels. The AutoCAD design of the cylinder by the designer and architects Bo Bendtsen and Wood and Marsh was translated into a compositional score, defining elements of duration and repetition. Sections of the actual steel of the scaffold, and the exoskeleton of the cylinder became sound sources for extensive computer treatment and alteration. A variety of contact and ambient mikes were used in the field recordings which then underwent extensive treatment in the ABC studios. The City Link pollutant cylinder was sounded before it became irrevocably silenced the moment the city link tunnel opened and the extractor fans, upon activation, become silenced with rooms of acoustic baffles. It is a sounding of an ephemeral acoustic space which was silenced at the moment of birth.

Diffusion: cylindrical speaker array in fixed position simulating listening inside the cylinder as architecture.
