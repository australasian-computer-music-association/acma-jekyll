bio: Julian Knowles is a composer/performer working with computers, sound synthesis, field recording and digital audio processing. His work spans studio and live performance, composition, and sound design for film, exploring the inherent (im)possibilities of various audio manoeuvres and uncovers the musicality of unlikely sound sources. He synthesises elements from an eclectic range of sonic practices such as acousmatic music, film sound, location recording, pop music production techniques, surround sound and audio dsp. As a solo performer, Julian has toured the UK, USA, Latin America and Korea to critical acclaim and is also a member of the group Social Interiors (Extreme Records). He is currently a Senior Lecturer and Electronic Arts/Music Technology Co-ordinator at the University of Western Sydney.

Aural navigations, slo-core laptop electronics, deep dsp, time lapse field recordings - a seething mass of unfolding tones, drones and cinematic audiospaces... Sleeper represents an ongoing engagement with the laptop as musical instrument. Processed field recordings and pure synthesised audio collide and interweave to form complex sonic narratives. One of my principal compositional aims is to uncover the inherent musicality in found and captured audio, distilling materials from its raw complex state via subtractive, or filtering processes, to uncover hidden harmonic structures and pitch sets. Further processes are undertaken whereby these distilled forms of captured audio come into contact with synthesised audio and ambiguous sonic relationships are explored. In a sense I am inviting the listener to listen ‘beneath the surface’ of the sound as well as inviting the listener to disassociate sounds from their sources in order to liberate these materials in the musical realm. The work presented in this concert is an excerpt from the much larger 45’00” work.
