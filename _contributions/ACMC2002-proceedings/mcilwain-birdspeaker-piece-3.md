The Birdspeaker Pieces are a series of works that are generated by the SNet software. This software employs a behavioural-based model of biological neural nets. It is designed to generate spatio-temporal patterns by allocating audio events to 16 independent audio channels (this can be reduced to 8 or 4 channels if necessary). The software was developed by Peter Mcilwain who is Lecturer in Composition at the School of Music – Conservatorium at Monash University.
