Water Baby is inspired by my long term fascination with water and all its sonic possibilities. The journey began in June last year when I performed an improvisation at the Make It Up Club using a saucepan of water with clarinet and extension pipe. The sounds I discovered are the basis for the clarinet part. The pre-recorded material was developed with everyday water sounds such as filling up the sink and pulling out the plug, blowing bubbles, splashing in the rain, and water gushing through a down-pipe. The live clarinet, bass clarinet and water is manipulated live with a variety of reverbs, loops and pitch shifters. It is an improvised theatrical soundscape.
