The title [Poong-Gyeong] means Korean wind-bell(풍경 ⾵風磬). 'Poong-Gyeong' is a small bell that announces the wind and guests under the eaves or door. 'Poong-Gyeong' is generally known as a bell for blessing. I hope this sound will bring you many blessings after the pendemic times. This is for 4-channel fixed media. It premiered in The New York City Electro Acoustic Music Festival 2021 and has since been performed in SICMF and DICMF, among other places.
