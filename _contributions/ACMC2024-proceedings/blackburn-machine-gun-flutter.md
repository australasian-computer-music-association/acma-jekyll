The Phoenix rises and falls from the ashes of devastation, the cycle repeats as often is as necessary. A burnt field was once corrupt. Bass-tones and rising melodic materials provide the soundtrack for an undulating, oscillating, vacillating, and conceptually confronting take of machine operated violence. The composer describes his electroacoustic aesthetic as cyber film noir. -.. . .- - .... (death) .-- .- .. - ... (waits) -.-. --- -- . ... (comes) .-- --- . ... (woes) ... .. -. --. ... (sings) -.. . .- - .... (death) MaxMSP is used to coordinate live performer and machine. This piece calls for heavy use of the flutter tonguing technique by the wind performer.
