The soundscape composition “Underwater” is the portrait of a landscape poised between the imaginary and the real with its ears immersed in water. What does or could the world sound like while underwater? The composition was created using exclusively recordings made with a hydrophone immersed in the waters of the Tiber river in Umbria. These recordings are present both original and manipulated. The structure of the composition is a slow crescendo in which natural sounds are slowly deconstructed until they are devoured and overwhelmed by a distorted mass: it is the perennial relationship between the dynamics of nature and those of man. A relationship in which these two entities contrast and merge at the same time. In fact, the beginning and end of the piece coincide, natural life and human action are shown enclosed within a cycle: they are born and die together.
