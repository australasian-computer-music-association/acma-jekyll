My contribution presents the theme of diversity addressed to the sea, which is presented in its dark and cruel form, rather than as an element of natural beauty. The sea also has a darkness that drags with it deaths during shipwrecks and accidents of various kinds, even if it is always described as something fabulous (and surely it is), unfortunately there is also a dark side.
