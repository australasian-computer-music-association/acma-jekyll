Bayn el-gabal we el-bahr, ana // tra la montagna e il mare, io is an acousmatic piece revolving around voices and their relationship with bodies and collective rituals. It uses field recording as the constitutive raw material, though manipulated into opaque sounds through techniques like granular synthesis, and time stretching.
