As testing a new song writing approach with my band members and creating this longer form song for this project, I had an idea to start a “game” which I am going to start a background of a story and each band member will unfold and extend the story with a bit more extra information more and more. While the story unfolds, we improvised, discussed, and created some music ideas which fit the story progress. In this way, each band member participates in the design of the story and design of the sound which suitable for the story progression, each of us take turns to be the music director for certain periods of the whole story writing and music improvisation sessions. We called our project <The Voyage>. It is a science fiction story follows an astronaut recruited for a secret NASA mission aimed at discovering a parallel universe with other team members of this mission. Scientists have hypothesized that our current universe is a projected reality, which prevents us from uncovering the truth about existence and finding other planets with carbon-based life, which is why our universe is growing and made us harder to travel and reach out to the other planet and galaxy. Throughout our story progression we have jammed out basically 7 main song ideas which we may be able to link them together into a longer form of song or alternatively make them into a concept cinematic background music album.
