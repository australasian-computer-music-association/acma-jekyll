This is an electroacoustic work built up from sounds produced with different household objects and processed using delays, filters, and spectral techniques. The sounds are arranged in 3D space and rendered in higher order Ambisonics. The title makes reference to the feeling of being at once digitally connected to the whole world, but also completely physically isolated and with no real contact with people, close to the whole world, but at the same time situated nowhere at all.
