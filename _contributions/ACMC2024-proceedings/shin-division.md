This song has four scenes. Each of the three scenes has a different subject matter, and the last scene recombines the elements of the first and second scenes that precede. The first scene emphasized the mysterious atmosphere by adding high and scattered metallic sounds in various dark sounds. The second scene created tension by continuing to produce drone sounds, and added something like the sound of wind in between. The third scene used click noise as an element to completely contrast the other three scenes. It starts similar to the introduction of the second scene. In addition, It is characterized by repeating the metallic sound of the first scene at a fast tempo, and the sound of the wind comes out repeatedly in reverse and progress. Overall, I tried to make it feel like a movie that I couldn’t see.
