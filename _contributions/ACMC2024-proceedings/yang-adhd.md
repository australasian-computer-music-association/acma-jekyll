My creative practice is focused on the intersection of music, technology, and interactivity. I am interested in exploring new ways of creating and experiencing music that go beyond traditional performance practices. Through my work, I aim to create immersive and engaging audio-visual experiences that invite audiences to interact with the music in new and exciting ways. The theme of this work development is ADHD. Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is the most common neuro-developmental disorder of childhood. The main visual design of the work is a mirrored abstract line graphic. The graphics mimic the abstract feelings that people with ADHD experience in their daily lives of inability to focus, impulsivity, anxiety, and forgetfulness.
