This performance focuses on the musical functions of the Yangtze River gorges boatmen's songs and their application within the context of East-West musical fusion. Through a selection of seven pieces, the performance showcases the unique blend of traditional Chinese musical elements with Western music styles, emphasizing the historical and cultural significance of boatmen's songs as traditional labor chants. The program includes new arrangements of Chinese mountain songs, Mongolian music, and boatmen's songs, aiming to reinterpret these traditional melodies through modern musical language. Additionally, original compositions will be created based on the characteristics of Chinese folk music, exploring their expressive forms within contemporary contexts. Through this East-West fusion, the audience will experience the dialogue between tradition and modernity, as well as the mutual influence of diverse cultures. This performance is not only a tribute to traditional music but also a reflection on its sustainable development in contemporary society, aiming to inspire the audience's awareness and enthusiasm for cultural heritage.
