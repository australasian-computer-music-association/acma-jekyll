Indie music is renowned for its artistic innovation and experimentation, but the specific elements that define creativity in this genre are not fully understood. This study aims to decode and analyze the rhythms in indie music using a data-driven approach, focusing on rhythmic parameters such as tempo, rhythmic variation, and drum pattern complexity. By analyzing a large dataset of indie songs with advanced music analysis software, we applied statistical techniques and machine learning to identify significant patterns that distinguish indie music from other genres. The results indicate that indie music tends to have greater tempo variation and more complex drum patterns compared to other music genres. These findings support the hypothesis that rhythmic variation and complexity are key factors in the perception of creativity in indie music by listeners and critics. The conclusions of this research provide new insights into the rhythmic elements that contribute to creativity in indie music, enriching the understanding of the technical aspects of this genre and laying the foundation for further studies on how rhythmic variation and complexity influence listeners' perception of musical creativity.
