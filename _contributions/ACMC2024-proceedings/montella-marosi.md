“Marosi” wants to bring into the compositional space the phenomenal complexity of a few moments of a soundscape: the construction of a natural sonic event that is both predictable and unexpected. From this process, long vectors and percussive actions spring, the first which live of rationally describable parabolas and the latter, on the contrary, which is frenetic, unfinished, and interrupted. These two elements, the line, and the point - stealing from Kandinsky - develop on varied spatial plains that are sometimes only cartographically represented and uninhabitable, while some others are profoundly realistic.
