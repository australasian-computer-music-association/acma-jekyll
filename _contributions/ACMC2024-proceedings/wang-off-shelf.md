"Off-the-Shelf" is a 15-minute performance featuring a trio of human and AI collaborators. The performance includes a human electronic musician using a generative AI system to control a compact synthesizer (such as the MakeNoise 0-COAST, Korg Volca FM, among other instruments we have experimented with) and a second human electronic musician playing on a separate synthesizer while interacting with the AI's actions in a mixed reality space. The AI musical agent, embodied as a virtual keyboard interface, visualizes its musical actions alongside the human musician’s instrument, redefining co-creative artificial musical experiences. The motivation behind this performance is to advance live performance practices that incorporate human musicians and AI systems in real-time collaboration. Despite continuous advancements in technical complexity and capability in intelligent creative systems, there remains a need to explore their integration in live settings. We have showcased the "Off-the-Shelf" system in three configurations and will feature it at the Artificial Intelligence and Musical Creativity 2024 conference. The first configuration involved an improvised recording session with the AI system controlling a MakeNoise 0-COAST synthesizer. The second was a public concert where the AI system was connected to a Korg Volca FM using the GenAI MIDI Plug. The third configuration was another public concert featuring the AI system on a Microfreak synthesizer, coperforming with a mixed reality instrument. Attached to this submission is the recording of the second performance, demonstrating the setup and approach that will be used at ACMC, with an additional human performer.
