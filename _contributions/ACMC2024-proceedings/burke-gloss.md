Gloss is inspired by a series of nine pen and ink drawings, which were used as the graphic notation for different instrumental combinations, electronics and visuals. The nine graphics were then integrated into the video artwork which shows glimpses of the graphics in superimposed layers in conjunction with pencil drawings of squiggles depicting wire as seen in the score. The depiction of wire glosses the image with a superficial lustre. Each graphic in Gloss from the nine pen and ink drawings is interpreted by both acoustic sound and live/prerecorded electronics and then combined with a series of black and white drawings, shadow line drawings and pencil drawings on paper formed into squiggles depicting wire, which were then photographed. The aim was to take the sonic world to the next level of real-time interaction in which compositional decisions are made continually through the graphic notations in the score during the performance. Many random computer samples open the free improvisational shaping from performance to performance. Each performance is thus a snapshot of exploration of the combination of acoustic instrumentalists, live electronics and graphic notations. Gloss may be “staged” rather than presented “in concert” in different parts of the space, also with multiple projections of the images if the space permits.
