I propose a thirty-minute lecture/recital to show how I developed the Future Research section of my PhD thesis into a book of timekeeping beats, phrases and groove concepts based on quintuplet and septuplet subdivisions. The book establishes a rhythmic framework (and “personal pedagogy”) for recalibrating 8th and 16th-note vernacular archetypes of Funk/R&B drumming to form tuplet- based analogs of those patterns. Specifically, this involves staggering the subdivisions of traditional “straight” 8th and 16-note grooves based on a system of “pivots frames”, while maintaining conventional quarter-note pulse and backbeat structures. The presentation will outline the basics of this method using notation (as below), sound files and live demonstrations.
