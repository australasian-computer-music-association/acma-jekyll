This is an eight minute improvisational piece using Wilsonic Software controlling the Arturia CZ V software synthesizer and the Arturia Delay Eternity effects unit. The scale used is a 21 note just intonation scale – a Pentadic Diamond based on harmonics 1, 3, 5, 7 and 11. The Pentadic Diamond is a structure based on the work of the late Ervin Wilson, Mexican-American music theorist. The Wilsonic Software is the product of Marcus Hobbs and others. The piece is a live keyboard improvisation exploring some of the harmonic worlds available in this scale. One chord that occurs throughout the piece is a 5 note chord based on 1/1, 5/4, 11/8, 3/2, and 7/4, which functions as a kind of tonic which is freely approached and moved away from. Aug 25 24, Warren Burt
