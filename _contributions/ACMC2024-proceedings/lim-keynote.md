In this talk, Monica will discuss undisciplinarity as her approach to art-making. In an age of increasing specialisation, many artists are resisting self-discipline, instead bringing a level of unknowingness and willingness to learn to every project. This allows for creative ideation and collaboration that reflect the complexities of a world and being in the world that is fuzzy and constantly shifting. However, is it madness to invent new forms of engagement for every situation (Gaver 2020)? Is it possible to maintain a sustainable undisciplined practice? More importantly, how is undisciplinarity different from mediocrity?
