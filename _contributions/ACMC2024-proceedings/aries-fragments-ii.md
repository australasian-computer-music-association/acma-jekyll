Fragments II explores the dynamic relationship between sounds generated by a modular synthesizer. By analyzing overtone structures in real time, it audibly shifts timbre. Field recordings serve as the composition's foundation, contrasting with generative, sequenced patterns to create organic transitions that evolve into new drones before receding. Four static and four moving loudspeakers spatialize sounds, creating resonances and delay patterns within the performance space. Fragments II aims to create a minimal yet complex sound world, blending contemporary electronica, club, and synthesizer influences with an experimental approach to contemporary music.
