"I Am Not My Body" is a multi-channel composition designed for voice and electronics, resulting from a collaborative effort between an all-female choir and an electronic music composer. Inspired by the ever-evolving landscape of Artificial Intelligence, the piece delves into the exploration of the human voice within a dynamic multichannel environment, incorporating numerous layers of recorded voices. The composer aimed to create an immersive sonic experience, enveloping the listener with the richness of the choir's diverse vocal textures. Through the strategic use of rhythms and hockets, the piece unfolds seamlessly in the multi-channel format, enhancing the spatial dimensions of the sound. Originally conceived and spatialised in the 16-channel ARUP studio in Sydney, the composition took advantage of the studio's capacity to exploit vertical space as an integral element of the presentation. For this particular performance, the piece will be adapted for an 8-channel configuration, maintaining its essence while catering to the unique characteristics of the chosen setup. "I Am Not My Body" invites audiences to contemplate the intersection of human expression and technology, fostering a thought-provoking dialogue through its innovative sonic exploration. The piece is to become part of an electronic opera on Artificial Intelligence where the live performers will perform with the multichannel speaker array.
