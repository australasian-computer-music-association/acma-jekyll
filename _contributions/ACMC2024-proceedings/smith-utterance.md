Utterance, for 8-channel fixed media, combines a variety of sound sources prominent in both western (trumpet, clave, and ukulele sounds) as well as non-western (African djembe and shakers, Native American water drums, and Tuvan throat singing techniques) music making. These musical identities, however, are not the only sources used. Everyday objects and sounds are used equally and treated as motivic material, creating a vast array of sounds and environments impossible to capture in the real world. It is in this imaginary space that a new culture emerges from the building blocks of our combined musical heritages. A culture that is summoned, materializes, and dissipates just as quickly as it is formed.
