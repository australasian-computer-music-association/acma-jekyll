Silence explores a specific anxiety symptom characterized by hypersensitivity to normal sounds, amplifying them to the point of discomfort. For those experiencing this state, every minor noise becomes harsh and overwhelming, triggering irritability, fear, and anxiety. This can lead to rapid heartbeat, shortness of breath, muscle tension, and even extreme fatigue and mental collapse. The piece is divided into two parts. The first part, "Noise Attack," features fragments of everyday noises (like electric currents and metallic clashes) that are amplified in the sufferer's ears, creating an overwhelming torrent of sound. These sounds build to a point of suffocation, reflecting the inescapable nature of the experience. The second part, "Silence," contrasts the noise with moments of calm and serenity, interspersed with intense and erratic soundscapes. It illustrates the sufferer's struggle to find peace amid the noise. As the music progresses, the calm begins to break down, symbolizing the ongoing battle against external noise and internal anxiety. I utilized various sampler techniques to create the "Noise Attack," transforming ordinary sounds into unpredictable, threatening elements. Sound fragments like metal clashes are stretched, shifted, and inverted, creating an ever-changing and disorienting soundscape. For the vocal processing, effects like Vocal Synth, Echo, Tremolo, and Vocal Transformer give the voice an electronic texture, representing the distorted and amplified inner turmoil of an anxiety sufferer. These effects capture the intense sensitivity and helplessness associated with anxiety, allowing the listener to feel the psychological torment of this heightened auditory perception.
