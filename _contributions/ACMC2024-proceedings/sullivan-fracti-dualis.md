Fracti Dualis, Latin for “Broken Dual”, is a multimedia installation piece exploring themes of identity and the inherent dualities that pervade within oneself. These dualities manifest in the way we interpret memories, the struggle between what is moral & immoral, and the ever- looming shadow self. The concept of this project was consequential of my own duality of practice; I perform electronic dance music under the alias of Fox Bailey (an idyllically eccentric character I have created) and have also, in recent times, begun to create conceptual, more personally relevant audiovisual works, like this project, under my birth name Dylan Sullivan. The intersection between these two compositional identities is a core feature of this piece that is pervaded through abstract imagery, semiotic relations and juxtaposing musical/sonic material. A visual motif that pervasively engages this concept is the reoccurrence of mirrors and reflections throughout the piece. This is representative of the deep introspection that is involved in the exploration of one’s dualistic self. Through visual and sonic material inspired by the artistic movements that is glitch art and glitch music, the fracturing of one’s own identity that is experience so commonly in our modern world is brought to the forefront. I invite you to listen to this music and view these visuals in tandem as the visual channels are split between two screens. You are also invited to engage in the liminal space between the two worlds/screens and explore which side takes your focus more closely. This might reveal where your own hidden dualistic intentions lay.
