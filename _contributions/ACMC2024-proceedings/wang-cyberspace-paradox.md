"Cyberspace Paradox" explores the intersection of human vocal expression and technological innovation through the use of Open Sound Control (OSC) in real-time sound manipulation. This work examines the concept of "openness" within composition and performance, leveraging OSC technology to dynamically control and transform live and pre-recorded vocal elements. Utilizing wireless touchscreen devices and a custom interface, the piece allows for the fluid modulation of sound, blending live vocals with pre-composed structures and processed audio tracks. Through the integration of OSC, the work delves into the transformative capabilities of the voice, aiming to expand its expressive potential in a digitally mediated music environment. The real-time manipulation of the human voice in combination with technology highlights a futuristic approach to vocal performance, transcending traditional boundaries and opening new avenues for creative exploration. Thematically, the work aligns with ACMC 2024's focus on "futurity" by demonstrating how cutting-edge technologies like OSC can redefine the future of vocal performance, bridging organic and computational sound worlds in ways that challenge both traditional and contemporary music-making practices.
