"In The Name Of The MOON" is a series of interactive multichannel pieces loosely based on characters from the iconic anime franchise "Sailor Moon." Each piece is an immersive and interactive character sketch of one of Sailor Moon's closest friends and allies: Sailors Jupiter, Mars, Mercury, and Venus. Vague and seductive sound-images of light, thunder, and water contrast with sonic waves of fire, warmth, and emotional intensity. Created in SuperCollider, these pieces can be presented in either four or eight channels. Each piece is comprised of elaborate patterns and meta-patterns of sound, which can be triggered and de-triggered as desired by listeners (clicking on colorful and textually suggestive interface buttons). Each piece is intentionally limited in generative sources, relying on elaborate interactions between noise and harmonics, shifting delay and reverb, intentional glitches and highfrequency panning to create an endless kaleidoscope that can be constantly manipulated by participants, or allowed to unfold indefinitely without any button-clicking at all. These pieces can be presented as a low-maintenance installation, featuring any one or all of the pieces in the series, or also be presented in a multichannel concert format, with audience members "pressing" buttons by pointing with laser pointers to a projected screen.
