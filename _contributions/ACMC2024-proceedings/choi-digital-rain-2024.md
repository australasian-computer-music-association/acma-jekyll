The sound of rain can give us a sense of security but also evoke the fear of death. Rain is a symbol of life that saves plants and animals but also symbolizes purification and death when floods occur. In this way, rain is both the sound of life and death and contains a deep meaning that stimulates our spiritual imagination. In this project, I tried to create various textures similar to the sound of rain through various sound processes using the sound of flowing water recorded in a lake and create an ambient musical narrative of the continuous changes in those sounds. Although not the actual sound of rain, these sound textures are abstract objects that convey symbolic images of rain. The title of the piece is borrowed from Alain Resnais' Last Year in Marienbad, where the words and the memory that contains them distort, filter, corroborate, and even construct each other, resulting in the observation that “conversation took place in a vacuum, as if the words meant nothing, as though they could have no meaning. A sentence would be suspended in space, frozen in its flight, and then could resume its journey there or elsewhere." All sonic materials are derived from a vibrato F above middle C played on the concert flute, which serves as the metaphor for couloirs, both for their resemblance as well as the fact that they are essentially filters—couloirs being the filter for the memory in the film and the flute being the filter of the air and musical note. The piece can be regarded as a process of excavating from the flute various words and voices that speak and sing incomprehensibly but are nevertheless full of expression.
