A reverberating wave, reminiscent of an echoing countryside sea, reminds us from 300 Cairo’s minarets to prioritize prayer over sleep. This mesmerizing scene was captured at 5:28 AM on February 9, 2023, from ADEF's rooftop in Cairo's Muqattam neighborhood. Our gaze delimits the field of art. Field recording, as an operation determined by rituals, times, and techniques, qualifies for its non-exhaustive character by releasing the need to be artistically varied. Thus escaping the source into the source itself. In this perspective, the world seems to arrange itself as a true musical paradigm that dictates its syntax, its connections, and its structural functions. This composition almost exclusively of recordings made in Cairo during the month of February 2023, using only the Zoom H2n recorder.
