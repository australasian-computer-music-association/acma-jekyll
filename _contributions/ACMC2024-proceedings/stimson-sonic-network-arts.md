Sonic Network Arts Performance is a live/electronic performance that will include performers from the United States, Taiwan, and Austria, connecting in real time in an online, networked performance. We will use traditional instruments from various traditions, custom instruments, and various realtime electronic transformations of these instruments, along with realtime visual processing to create a work that re-imagines sounds in spaces and the transformations of space and sound.
