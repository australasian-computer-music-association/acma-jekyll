Construction in ENSO is a live audiovisual performance. A variety of models related to climate are used to generate data and presented as sound and moving image in realtime via audiovisualisation, the simultaneous sonification and visualisation of the same source of data. All sounds are derived through the direct transposition of data to the audible range i.e. audification in the context of auditory display, or non-standard synthesis in the context of music as used by composers such as Xenakis. The same data sources are used at longer time-scales akin to parameter mapping sonification in the context of auditory display, or algorithmic composition in the context of music, which result in pitch, rhythm and phrases to further determine the parameters for the audification/non-standard synthesis.
