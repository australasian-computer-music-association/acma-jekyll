Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become increasingly popular and accessible in various domains of music creation. It has shown the ability to generate lyrics, mix and master music, synthesise and mimic timbre, reimagine popular music, and even generate entirely new songs. Despite this, in recent years there has been a paucity of qualitative studies surrounding individuals interacting with these tools. Through an artist talk I aim to help alleviate this paucity by providing insight to how our roles as music creators have been affected by this technological shift and how future generations may adopt this technology. This artist talk uses an autoethnographic approach to document my experience interacting with various AI tools for a range of musical tasks within my practice. This talk discusses which of these tools I have adopted into my practice and how AI has affected my view of music creation.
