dwell in our world

The Materialised Temporality of Dust is an ongoing research project that explores dust as a material and metaphorical device to situate and critique temporality, and the way we narrate and investigate the past and future, from a non-human, microbial point of view. The most recent output from the project is an immersive VR collaboration between Carolina Ramirez-Figueroa, Antony Nevin, Campbell Orme, Laura Selby, and Neil Crispin Aldridge, which showed at Ars Electronica 2024. Quiet, Distorted is an octophonic sound piece by Neil Crispin Aldridge and Laura Selby exploring the sporulation phase as the multitude of microbial inhabitants become dormant to survive unfavourable conditions. In this crepuscular state time stretches out, independent of ages, and the microbial life sit contemplating futurity — biding their time until the moment is right…
