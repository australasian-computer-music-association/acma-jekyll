Phonosests are the equivalent of palimpsests in the context of sound recordings. In a phonosest, one can hear a pre-existing sound recording amidst a new one, no matter how extreme the transformation. For the current purposes, I present three phonosests all based on the same hypophonogram (i.e. a pre-existing sound recording):

1. Surface-Substance

2. Sparks

3. Error

4. Hypophonogram [Excerpt]

As each phonosest unfolds, the presence of the hypophonogram becomes more literal.
