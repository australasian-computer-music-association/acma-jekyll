Hollow Point is a fixed media piece investigating transformational techniques on various separation techniques. Recordings are separated into individual layers through a variety of automated processes. Individually, these layers can often be described as sounding “thin” or “hollow”, often containing artifacts of the other layers. When all the layers are placed together, these artifacts and imperfections will cancel out with the other layers, resulting in the original source audio file.
