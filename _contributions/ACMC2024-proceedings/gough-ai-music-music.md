Generative ai is defined by the Department of Education (AUS) as including the generation of “new content” such as “audio” yet the surrounding discourse remains focus on “text”. Whilst generative ai in educational settings is relevant contemporary topic, most of the discourse refers (in large part) to text-based generative outcomes. Generative ai art and music is itself a hot topic in academic and popular media alike, yet, there is a paucity of research of generative ai in education settings with a focus on music media. How do educators design assessment frameworks that is inclusive of student engagement with generative music ai? How do students approach modes of original practice that is inclusive of generative music ai? How do institutions design syllabi that is responsive to a future that is inclusive of generative music ai?
