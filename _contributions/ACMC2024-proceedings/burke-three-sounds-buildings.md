This work is based on vivid impressions or dreams of buildings, train rides, experiences I had in Canberra and travelling Melbourne Australia and with glimpses of Prague. The work was influenced by a series of drawings and etchings of buildings I conceived in 1982 that stand austere and grey but as I worked on the composition away from Canberra the buildings of Melbourne in 2005 became more transparent so the composition began to take on a more human approach. The clarinet and electronics use many extended techniques filter filters taking on an improvisational manner with energy, flamboyance and focus throughout that are in conversation with the electronic effects of the affected clarinet.
