This piece was commissioned by the trombonist Simone de Haan. Martin Wesley-Smith was also always there with technical help. The title refers to a short story by the Argentinian author Jorge Luis Borges in which Nature is likened to a fearful sphere, quivering 'whose centre is everywhere and circumference is nowhere' The work was created on the Fairlight CMI using Music Composition Language in the CMI Studio at the Sydney Conservatorium between 1986-1988.
