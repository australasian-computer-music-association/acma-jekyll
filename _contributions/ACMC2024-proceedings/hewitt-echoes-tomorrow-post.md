In this experimental musical collaboration, Associate Professor Donna Hewitt and Mnasle Bass come together to explore the concept of futurity through the lens of improvised sound and the post digital layering of musical technology in combination with Artificial Intelligence. Mnasle manipulates the deep, resonant frequencies of the bass, blending them and looping them in Ableton, while Donna captures and transforms vocal textures using an E mic, also processed and layered in Ableton. The performance is largely improvised, allowing the artists to respond to each other in real time, creating a dynamic and ever-evolving soundscape. This approach reflects the unpredictability and fluidity of the future—a future that is shaped by both human intuition and technological intervention. A unique element of this collaboration is the incorporation of AI-generated lyrics. Using "futurity" as the thematic prompt, an AI algorithm generates spontaneous lyrics for the vocal loops, enhancing the improvisational nature of the performance. This fusion of human artistry and machine intelligence deepens the exploration of the post-digital aesthetic, where technology becomes an active participant in the creative process. The result is a sonic journey that challenges the boundaries between the analog and the digital, the human and the machine. It is a meditation on the possibilities of what music— and by extension, the future—can become when we embrace the unknown and the yetto-be-imagined.
