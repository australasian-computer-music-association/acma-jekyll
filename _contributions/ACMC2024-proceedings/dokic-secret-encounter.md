Secret Encounter’ represents an approach to musical hybridity incorporating elements of electroacoustic music and Mediterranean world music. In particular, this composition uses author’s world music piece as a material where electroacoustic music techniques are applied. The aim was to create a musical story about a secret encounter between East and West on the deck of the ship in the middle of the Mediterranean Sea. Therefore, this composition incorporates elements of musical heritage from different Mediterranean countries where the main protagonist is viola which encounters Eastern Mediterranean vocals in the second half of the composition. This project is created in Ableton Live using Max for Live device Granulator II. The sounds of the original source are reconstructed using sampling and granular synthesis techniques such as time shrinking, time stretching, granular reverberation, cloud coalescence and time doubling to create complex sonic textures, layers and atmospheres. In the process of granulation of sampled sound and perceptual processing, the sound of viola was divided into short enveloped grains or 'phonemes' as suggested by Barry Truax. While using the time stretching technique to enhance the perceived magnitude of the sound of viola, the fundamental timbral connection with the soloist is maintained. This technique allows viola solo part to retain its recognizability, against which the sound of ambient background parts, extended beyond its ability to be recognized, is placed. Finally, instead of following the electro-acoustic music foundation on timbre manipulation, this composition follows Paul Lansky’s techniques focused on jazz harmony that he applied to his albums ‘More Than Idle Chatter’ (1994) and Idle Chatter Junior (1999).
