We introduce the concept of Synaespatia as the experience of sound and music within the spatial domain using the technologies of a Networked Music-Spatial Virtual Environment (NMSVE). The NMSVE operates from the technological confluence of digital musical instrumentation and virtual reality-based spatialisation: the capacities of each instrument are extended to enable explorations in sound (through vast synthesised virtual instrument soundscapes) and space (through a parametrically-adaptable virtual environment). For the performer, new modes of 'musico-spatial' performance are enabled, where a digital drum kit or keyboard transforms to an instrument capable of generating vast electronic soundscapes and, concurrently, spectacular spatialisations in virtual reality. For the audience, this unique musico-spatial performance is exhilarating. Using a gaming head-mounted display or inside a VR CAVE, users can experience 'Synaespatia': sound and music are experienced in real-time as dynamic and spectacular spatialisations in VR, enabled by the affordances of the NMSVE. Musical notes are translated as 3D VR spatial objects and the parameters of sound are projected inside a 'Sound Tunnel' in VR. We propose that the technologies of the NMSVE provide new interfaces for musical expression in four ways: through new modes of musico-spatial performance; through new modes of networked performance, through new experiences of sound and music as synaespatia and as a new means for the hearing-impaired and deaf community to actively participate in the joys of music and sound- through experiencing synaespatia and through new-found capacities of interacting with musical instruments for musicspatial performance in both a solo capacity and through jamming with musicians.
