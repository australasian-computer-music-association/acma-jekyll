'NRV: out in mysterious radio-space, lonely beacons tirelessly sing in deep drones of data: in this case, the call-sign 'NRV'. These transmissions are not intended for human ears: machine speaks unto machine. What are they saying? They seem to waste their sweetness on the midnight air...'
