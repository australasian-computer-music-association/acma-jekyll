I am a percussionist specializing in performing pieces with technology. My interests are preserving and developing a performance practice for pieces that use electronic media or multimedia, and creating a method for integrating technology and music pedagogy. In the future I hope to develop more robust signal analysis tools for percussionists, creating performance realizations of past works and continuing further development of artistic and pedagogic relationships between music and technology.
