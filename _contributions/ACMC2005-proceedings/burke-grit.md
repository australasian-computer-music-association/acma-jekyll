Set in both the coastline of Victoria, Australia and the city of Inchagaya, Tokyo, Grit brings two cultures together to create a timeless scene. The clarinet is aimed to mimic the attack of the Indian drum parts. Its musical context is based on improvisations and experimentation using frequency changes. The visuals and Indian drum imitate the clarinet as if in conversation and is lyrically thick with the percussive sounds overlapping themselves to form complex rhythms and visual layers of paintings worked on in tissue paper, glass, fish themes, water and line drawings.
