Iron Emerald is largely based on recordings of pieces of metal being activated by various methods, revealing different facets of the sound. An orchestra to be discovered in each piece of metal. Particle-based synthesis (using the FOG opcode in Csound) seemed to give access to the inner sound worlds of these recordings. Convolution (using Sound Hack) brought out resonant frequencies common to the different pieces and allowed unusual impulse responses to be applied.
