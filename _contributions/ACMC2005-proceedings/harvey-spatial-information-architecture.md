The SIAL Sound Studios were officially opened on December 3, 2004 with the inaugural SPECTRUM series of concerts. This followed an intensive period of development starting from early 2003 with the construction of a lab, multi-user space and offices, and ending with the completion of the Pod studio in August 2004.
