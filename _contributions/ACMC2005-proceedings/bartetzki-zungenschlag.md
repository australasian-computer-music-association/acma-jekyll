The sounds that I have used in this piece are recordings of vibrating „tongues“.

In musical acoustics the German word „Zunge“ (tongue) is a basic term for single and double reeds in woodwinds, like oboe, clarinet or saxophone, as well as reeds or vibrating plates in the accordion, harmonica, jews harp or some organ pipes. Even the lips of a brass player or the human vocal cords can be regarded as „vibrating tongues“.

The resonance frequency of these reeds and lips determines some-times in connection with a coupled cavity or a tube, the pitch that we will hear. The vocal cords, the vocal tract and the tongue are the basis for the production of different vowels, consonants and varying pitches – physical fundament of speech and singing and thus of music.
