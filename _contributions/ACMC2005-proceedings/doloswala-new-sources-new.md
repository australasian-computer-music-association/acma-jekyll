The work involved using altitude data as a data source within a city topology. The piece submitted involved collecting numerous data readings from elevators within Sydney city buildings, both high rise and low and old and new. The objective was to build an aural landscape representing the journeys people make in the city. Journeys of the everyday, the working day. The equipment used for this was a Flytec Variometer, which is equipment more usually used in aerosports. These data streams were then extracted as comma separated data and processed through Audacity, Sound Forge and Acid programs.
