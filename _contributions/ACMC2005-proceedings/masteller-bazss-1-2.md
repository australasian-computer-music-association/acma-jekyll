bazss_1.2 is a continually evolving piece that first began in the fall of 2002. Parts 1 & 2 were created at the Hiller Computer Music Studios using a variety of sound processing and editing tools. The piece contains recordings of double bass and voice. Some of the samples were manipulated by means of spectral analysis / resynthesis / filtering, while others were granulated using standard time stretching techniques. All of the sounds were then spatialized using the software Max/MSP and assembled in ProTools. Thanks to Mike Basinski, Chris Fritton, Mark Karwan, and Leif Nicklas for the improvisations, from which all the samples came.
