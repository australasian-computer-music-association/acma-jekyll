The School is currently offering courses and subjects in composition at undergraduate and post-graduate levels. These courses, run by Dr Thomas Reiner and Peter Mcilwain, include tuition in electro-acoustic and computer music. The period from 2000 to 2001, has seen the development of several activities and initiatives that advance these areas of music at Monash.
