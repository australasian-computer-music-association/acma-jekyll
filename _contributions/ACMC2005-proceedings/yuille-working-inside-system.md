Working with IT in institutions poses specific issues like: centralisation of support and resources, locking down of administrator access to those resources etc These issues can lead to long term pedagogical problems, like planning curriculum around centralised time lines and the teaching of IT skills in humanities or design programs. This workshop will explore some models for 'working inside the system' by presenting a number of small case studies and brainstorming possible ways for academics and IT support to work together.
