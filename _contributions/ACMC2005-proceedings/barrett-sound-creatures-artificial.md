Sound Creatures utilises a combination of programmed Artificial Life models with prepared sound. Each creature has a personality type dictating the way in which it interacts with its micro world. Like silkworms to silk; the Sound Creatures weave a kind of concrete music through the process of movement, interaction, breeding, spawning, and death.

The Sound Creatures project was realised as part of the QUT Masters Program and the REV festival by Lloyd Barrett under the supervision of Andrew Brown. Lloyd Barrett has assisted with and curated Small Black Box, performed at SOOB and Liquid Architecture festivals and runs the Audiopollen weekly sound-art program, on 4ZZZ.
