Introducing a technology probe methodology to investigate sonifications in real-world sporting contexts… Cycling, Jogging, Aerobics, Kayaking, Aikido, Walking, … A platform for rapid iterative design, prototyping and trialling of interactive sonifications with feedback from athletes physically engaged in the sport. Recorded data and sound files can be used in a post process as well.
