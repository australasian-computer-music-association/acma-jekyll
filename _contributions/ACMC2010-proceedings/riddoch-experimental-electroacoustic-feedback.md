The paper presents a phenomenological description of an experimental approach to solo improvised electroacoustic performance utilizing acoustic space, a Logic Pro environment buss array, Max/MSP and inherently unstable acoustic, electronic audio and midi feedback loops. Thematic dissolution is the musical theme following notions of indeterminacy in the composition and performance of musically organized sound. Embodied, performative engagement within acoustic space, both conceptual and practical, passive and active, is discussed in relation to experimental composition, the technological setup, the sounds themselves and the audience.
