A selection of recordings form La Trobe Music's collection of tapes have been transferred to CD over the last year. We have tried to put together a mixture of materials on each CD consisting of works from 1976 to 1999, electroacoustic & instrumental/vocal. Most of the works are original pieces by staff and students, and the electronic works constitute about 50% of the works. There are now 10 CDs of material. Our object is to preserve some of the sounds from La Trobe for future listening & research and deposit 4 complete sets in 4 archives/libraries in Sydney, Canberra, & Melbourne.
