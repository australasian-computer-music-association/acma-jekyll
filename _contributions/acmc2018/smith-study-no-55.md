Study no. 55 explores the concept of malleable phase relationships for an ensemble of 5 to 15 performers. The term ‘Malleable Phase Relationships’ describes a rhythmic approach in which performers are not simply phasing with one another, but also moving in and out of phase with an imaginary time basis. The Animated Score features a series of generative no-theme variations, and may last for any duration.
