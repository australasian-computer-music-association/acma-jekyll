Fluctuant is an installation that explores the primitive functionality of sound in human perception. Naturally, we have an immediate tendency to look at a perceived vibrating source and to change our listening position accordingly. This sensory stimulus is challenged by exposing the listener to an immersive sound environment, which transforms itself and reacts against the listener’s sitting position.
