This paper provides preliminary results from a broad study on the diverse approaches to notating the infinite possibilities of environmental electroacoustic music. This broad study includes a series of case studies on works that integrate environmental soundscapes with electroacoustic music and aims to explore the diversity of methods composers are using today. This paper is primarily reporting on a survey that aims to outline the challenges and possibilities from the perspectives of both the composer and performer and identify methods to engage a wider interest and accessibility in performing mixed music today.
