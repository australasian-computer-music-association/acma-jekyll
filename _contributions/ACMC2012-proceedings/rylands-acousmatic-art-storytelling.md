In this presentation I will discuss my current ideas on the interplay between multi-channel acousmatic sound art and storytelling. I will describe my procedures for collecting sound recordings, designing loudspeaker arrays and installation approaches, and developing sound manipulation techniques for spatial sound environments.

Following this, I will talk about orality and storytelling in acousmatic composition. I will demonstrate examples in my work where I integrate the communicable voice with everyday, and abstract, sound objects and soundscapes.

I propose that the integration of storytelling with referential and abstract sound types can provide a basis for the presentation of personal contexts in which the material is unified by a clear and significant purpose.

I believe that the infusion of these materials contributes to a meaningful production process and greater communication with varied listeners.
