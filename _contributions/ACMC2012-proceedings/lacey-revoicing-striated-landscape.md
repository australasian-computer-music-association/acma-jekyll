Revoicing the Striated Soundscape is an 8-channel interpretation of a sound-artwork recently commissioned by the City of Melbourne Public Art Commission to be installed in a CBD laneway. Using onsite sounds the artwork repatterns a laneway soundscape creating an acoustic space in which air-conditioners speak, chant and sing to passers-by. It is the intention of the artwork to bring attention to the homogenising effects of air-conditioners and ventilation units on city soundscapes, and to indicate creative responses that attempt to diversify the listening experiences of city dwellers. The installation uses the software program WasP, an interactive performance environment for multichannel spatial soundscape composition, developed at SIAL Sound Studios.
