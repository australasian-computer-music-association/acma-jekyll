bio: |-
  Ros Bandt is an award winning international sound artist from Australia who is famous for her soundings and installations of unusual acoustic spaces from wheat silos, water tanks, limestonequarries, swimming pools, ancient deserts, historic buildings, and underwater environments. She has pioneered the use of endangered sounds in her sound installations and radiophonic works contributing to environmental and sound culture awareness, recording and interpreting Indigenous and multi-cultural languages in change, native wild life, old machinery and lost classical languages. She has built soundplaygrounds, interactive systems, global satellite performances, made sculptures, musical instruments and is an expert sound recordist and musician. She has interpreted sites and sound cultures worldwide including the Goatherd songs of Crete, the endangered sound of the Isobue, Japanese Sea Whistle, and the fleeting sounds of her own Aeolian harps and casuarinas in Lake Mungo. While living in Europe in 2010-11 she toured her international collaborative sound art performance with hair dye paintings with Johannes S Sistermanns Germany, Tracings CD Wergo, and composing and performing on Tarhu the Black Flacon CD with Erdem Helvacioglu, Pozitif Yappim, CD of the week in Istanbul. Her site specific electro-acoustic symphony, Medusa Dreaming was composed for the Yerebatan Sarayi underwater cistern in Istanbul, performing two concerts there with the Medusa ensemble she created for this event. She is a founding member of six ensembles incuding the early music ensemble La Romanesca with whom she is currently touring.

  She has been awarded Australia’s highest honours, the Don Banks Composers Award, The ABC sound artist in residence, the Sound Art Australia prize, and in the USA, she became the Inaugural Benjamin Cohen Peace fellow for her installation Altars of Power and Desire. Her commissions include the Paris Autumn Festival, Radio ORF Vienna, Transit, the Studio of Akustische Kunst WDR KOln, ABC, Melbourne International Festival, Ball State University USA, ReMap Athens and the Ionian University Kerkyra, Greece. The ensemble Charisma commissioned Blue Gold. She is published by Wergo, New Albion, EMI, Move Records, Sonic Gallery, Au Courant, L’agent des refuses, Cambridge Scholars Press, Fine Arts Press, The Australian Music Centre, Composers Autograph Limited edition and distributed by CDE Music and Indie Records.

  She founded the first online gallery of soundart in public space in Australia, www.sounddesign.unimelb.edu.au and has written several definitive books on sound art and culture, winning research awards for excellent scholarship at the University of Melbourne. Her PhD on performance practice of repetitive music was awarded from Monash University and she has since had three ARC grants. She lives from her art practice.

A new collaborative radiophonic work on Australian river ecology: a joint mix of river culture from the Murray River in Mildura Victoria to the rivers leading to Wolf Rock in Queensland. Rivers talk dissolves the dense local sonic profiles from the artists' solo works Voicing the Murray and Confluence in a dynamic profile of continuing issues surrounding river care and the sensitive man/nature relationships. Through the radiophonic medium of collaborative electroacoustic sound, these issues can be heard through the authentic local voices and revisited not only in Australia but worldwide.
