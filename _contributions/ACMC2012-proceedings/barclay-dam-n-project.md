Jobat Calling is a live and interactive audiovisual installation that connects Australian and Indian communities around a common concern: water security. The DAM(N) Project is a collaboration between director Jehan Kanga, vocal ensemble The Song Company, Bangalore based contemporary dance company Attakkalari, new/digital media company CuriousWorks and sound artist Leah Barclay. It presents the lives of remote communities in the Narmada Valley, displaced by large-scale dams developed to create power for Indian cities. The Project does not seek to engage in the heavily politicised issues around the Narmada, rather it tells stories taken from the many viewpoints and the living culture in the affected areas. The central idea within DAM(N) is the nature and mythology of water, its creative and destructive powers and our constant obsession to control it in the event of imminent scarcity. This is an issue that resonates with both the Indian and Australian psyche.
