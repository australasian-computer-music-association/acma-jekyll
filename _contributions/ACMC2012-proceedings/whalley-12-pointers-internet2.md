Using internationally linked high-speed broadband research networks (both IPv4 and IPv6) allows multiple bi-directional audio, video, OSC data, and graphics channels for real-time electroacoustic music composition/performance synchronously. Based on extensive work over the last three years from a research, performance and composition perspective (see Fields & Whalley 2012, for example), the presentation looks at twelve useful pointers others might consider when venturing into this area (Whalley 2012), as electroacoustic music studios become increasing connected real-time globally.
