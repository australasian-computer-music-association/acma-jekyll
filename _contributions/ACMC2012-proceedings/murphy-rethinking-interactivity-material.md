Solenoid-based drum beaters are the drum-striking method of choice in mechatronic percussion systems. While solenoids are simple both electronically and mechanically, they produce a highly nonlinear dynamic response: volume levels created by the beaters do not correspond with input values in a linear manner. The goal of this paper is to quantify this nonlinearity and discuss methods by which the composer and performer who works with such systems can, in the face of such nonlinearity, still create musical works with expressive dynamic range. Additionally, avenues for future work are discussed, as are methods for calibrating and retrofitting existing systems to behave in a more linear manner.
