Jam2jam XO is a software application developed for the One Laptop Per Child (OLPC) ‘XO’ laptop, an educational computer designed for use by children in poor or developing countries.

Jam2jam XO is one of family of jam2jam educational multimedia software instruments. While jam2jam XO adopts techniques used by the other jam2jam’s, jam2jam XO has been designed and developed to take particular advantage of the resources available on the XO laptop. The XO laptop boasts a number of innovative features, including wireless mesh networking and the ‘Sugar’ interface.

The jam2jam project holds a philosophy around teaching and education that shares much in common with principals held by the OLPC initiative. By developing a version of the jam2jam software for the OLPC XO laptop, jam2jam XO looks to further the objectives of both the jam2jam project and the OLPC initiative.

Having achieved over 30 000 downloads, jam2jam XO continues to be a popular activity used throughout the OLPC community.
