Jobat Calling is a live and interactive audiovisual installation which connects Australian and Indian communities around a common concern: water security. It presents the lives of remote communities in the Narmada Valley, displaced by large-scale dam development securing hydropower for Indian cities. It presents the lives of remote communities in the Narmada Valley, displaced by large-scale dams developed to create power for Indian cities. The central idea within DAM(N) is the nature and mythology of water, its creative/destructive powers and our constant obsession to control it in the event of imminent scarcity. This presentation will showcase the long-term contribution of creative industries can make to communities in struggle.
