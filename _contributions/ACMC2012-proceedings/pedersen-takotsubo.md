Takotsubo is an improvised Audio/Visual performance built around the interplay of three interfaces: traditional (guitar), the new (Kinect) and the ubiquitous (camera). These interfaces allow three different styles of interaction: the tactile and intended, the non-tactile and intended, and the non-tactile and unintended. This forms a tripartite interaction allowing varying and mutating degrees of agency and control for the participants (performers and audience).Each of these interactive systems affords different degrees of expertise and expression. In Takotsubo, the guitar, as traditional haptic music interface, provides fine and nuanced control to generate primary source audio material for the piece. Taking this material into a virtual spectral realm, it is then modified using live concatenative synthesis controlled by a Kinect-based gestural interaction system designed on principles of embodied music cognition. Finally, the audience, as participants in the performative moment, albeit without the same degree of agency as the performers, are included in the process via camera input into the generative audio system.
