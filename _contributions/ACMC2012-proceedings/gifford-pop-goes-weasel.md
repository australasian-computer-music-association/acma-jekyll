Pop Goes the Weasel is a proposed live performance of selected students works from the Queensland Conservatorium Popular Music Program’s third year course in Computer Music. The course revolves around the creation of a creative musical work, with a focus on interactivity. The best (and most interactive) student works will be selected for the performance, curated by Toby Gifford.
