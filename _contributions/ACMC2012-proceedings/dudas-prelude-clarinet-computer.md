This Prelude is the second in a series of interactive pieces for solo wind instrument with live computer processing. The piece explores (and blurs) the fine line between the sound of the live clarinet and the computer processed clarinet sound by the use of multiphonics in the clarinet part. The computer is also used to fill in the “missing” even harmonic partials in the clarinet’s odd-harmonic sound spectrum, morphing its sound into one resembling a saxophone. It is worth noting that all of the sounds in the electronic component of the piece come from the live performer on stage: sometimes the computer processes the live sound of the clarinet, and sometimes records some specific sounds for playback and processing later in the piece.
