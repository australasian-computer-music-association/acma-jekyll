bio: 'Dr.Young is a composer with interests in real-time computer music, interdiscipinary practice and performance. He was born in South Shields in 1968. He studied music at Oxford University and composition with John Casken & Peter Manning at Durham University, completing a PhD in 1995. He is a Senior Lecturer in Music at Goldsmiths, University of London in the Electronic Music Studios (EMS). Previously, he lectured at Oxford Brookes University and University of Wales, Bangor. His recent music has focussed on interactive/generative systems, exploring the interface between composed and improvised music-making. He is co-founder of the Live Algorithms for Music Network. He has worked with jazz musicians and improvisers as pianist, laptop musician & composer. [source: http://homepage.mac.com/oobop/myounginfo/about.html]'
