This paper describes a battery powered wireless interface focuses on free spatial movement for live musical interaction. The interface is a dodecahedron housing a specially designed 'brain' consisting of an Arduino connected to a direction-sensing sensor-fusion device offering nine degrees of freedom, twelve independently responsive contact microphones and twelve multi-colour leds. The outer surface of the dodecahedron serves as a functional physical input that allows the user to strike or press each of the faces whilst all directional movement of the device is simultaneously transmitted. Messages are converted to OpenSound Control(“OSC”) from a wireless receiver, and both hardware and software projects are developed continuing an inheritance of open-source licensed systems.
