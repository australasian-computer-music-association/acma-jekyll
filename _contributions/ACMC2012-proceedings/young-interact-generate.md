This workshop will explore the fundamental elements necessary in live algorithms theory: machine listening and algorithmic process for synthesis, which can be summarized as the “PfQ” functionality of a live algorithmic system.

In collaboration with fellow performer Brigid Burke, the workshop will include a demonstration of elements of the Max/MSP systems used in Michael Young’s recent compositions piano_prosthesis and Trio for Two Players.

Participants are welcome to bring along their own work where relevant to this topic, and as time permits, a hacking and/or discussion session will be included. Prior notice of interest in contributing would be appreciated: please contact Michael Young directly.
