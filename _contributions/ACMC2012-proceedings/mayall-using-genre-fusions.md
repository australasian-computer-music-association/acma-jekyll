Genre is a fundamental structuring force in our musical life. The name associated with a particular music can become a particular point of reference, not only for the sound and relationship between the compositional elements, but also for the social and cultural categories to which the musical image belongs. For someone attempting to determine the distinction between these categories, the problem arises that as new music is created, new genres are conceived to create a distinction from the preceding music. How can a composer use these fluid boundaries to explore a hybrid fusion of genres as a focus for original work? How will this work be approached by audiences? How can you be sure that a true interaction between the genres has taken place?
