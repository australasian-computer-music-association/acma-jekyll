This paper explores the role of electroacoustic composers in ecological crisis and introduces the Sonic Ecologies Framework, a multi-platform methodology to initiate cultural change through sound. The core of this methodology pivots on a site-specific creative project embedded in a multi-layered community cultural engagement process developed in response to a specific community. This evolving model is implemented by the artist, acting as an agent of change spiraling between contextualised theory and practice. This research presents results from a series of case studies implemented with this methodology and introduces the conceptual design of a major international project highlighting the future possibilities of this model.
