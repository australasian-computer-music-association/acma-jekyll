The timbres of Guitar and Flute sound best the tones of my July. Dappled sunlight, shimmering, pleasure, transience, quiet rain and a feeling of openness...... This is the first composition for flute, guitar and live electronics, and various extended techniques were explored in order to extract as much unique desirable timbres as possible. As a main compositional concern, creating organic sound relationship between musical instruments and computer was sought so that audience can be engaged. The electronic sound was created in the Max/ MSP programming environment, and has been renewed with each performance: as a primary change, three pre-recorded and modified sound clips were also included after an original performance, where all of the electronic sound was generated in real-time.
