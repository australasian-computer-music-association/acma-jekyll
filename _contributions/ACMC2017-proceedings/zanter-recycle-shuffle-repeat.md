Recycle+Shuffle+Repeat (2016) was commissioned by oboist Dr. Richard Kravchak for his U.S. tour 2016. The work recycles material from Homage (2014) for oboe and string orchestra and a FM synthesizer built in Max/MSP. Shuffling processes were applied at various levels working in conjunction with repeat, which was applied mainly to larger proportions using composer generated procedures. Clarinetist Brigid Burke, of Melbourne will premiere the clarinet version of this work.
