TRANSIT is about the stillness of the Lane ways of Melbourne in the early hours of the morning layered with a riot in the centre of Florence. The music/visuals depict the streets, people moving through both cities architecture and city scenes. TRANSIT explores connections with the present histories within the two cities and musical influence and the importance of colour painting, electronic beats, layered soundscapes and light to create a linkage between the two cities.
