This research describes an additive synthesis design for the didgeridoo which captures many of its subtle timbral and expressive characteristics. The design uses timbre morphing to model the dynamic spectra of the instrument in Csound. The design is very flexible, and allows vocal sounds, harmonic sweeps and flutter tonguing. Finally, a composed musical excerpt illustrates the expressive features of the design.
