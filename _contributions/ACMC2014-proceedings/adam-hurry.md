'Hurry' is a minute long 2-channel excerpt from a self-organising spatial-sound work entitled "Passing By… More Quickly". It is preoccupied with the sounds of motion, the motion of sounds and the relations and ambiguities that can arise. Courtesy of the RMIT University Sound Art Collection. Steve Stelios Adam has harboured a long-term fascination with music, sound and its associated technologies. As a composer and sound artist, Steve has created works for a variety of media and performed with instrumentalists, ensembles, choirs and interactive music systems of his own design. Informed by the technical challenges of his own works, he occasionally consults for other artists and organisations to develop software designs for specialised media-based projects.
