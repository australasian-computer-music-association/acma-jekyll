Katy Abbott is a Melbourne composer who looks for the absurd and quirky when setting text. Handsome (from the song cycle 'Words of Wisdom') is a quote from one of Who Magazine's 25 most beautiful people voted in 2004. Any guesses as to the identity? Performed by Richard Black of The Song Company.
