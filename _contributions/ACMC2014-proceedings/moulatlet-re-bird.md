re-bird is a recording of native birdlife made during a heavy rain event in inner-urban Melbourne, with minimal alteration and effects applied to the original captured sounds. Paul Moulatlet is a Melbourne-based composer whose works have been performed in Asia, Europe, the UK, the USA and numerous Australian events. Most of his works are for chamber ensembles and have been interpreted by leading performers including Trio Altrove 1.3 (Italy), Barrie Webb (UK), and noted Australian artists including Deborah Kayser, Ken Murray, Peter Neville, Carl Rosman, Eugene Ughetti and Tristram Williams.
