This is an excerpt from a fretless guitar improvisation using a microtonal scale comprising 1 qb2 qb3 4 qb5 qb6 qb7 1 with the quarter-tones being closer to Turkish 5 Koma flat rather than the equal temperament quarter-tone. Further AM processing was used creating a variety of sidebands and additional artifacts.
