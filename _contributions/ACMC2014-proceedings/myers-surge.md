Dr Peter Myers has taught music theory and composition at La Trobe University, the University of Melbourne, Monash University and Box Hill Institute where he was the Head of Music for five years. SURGE for two pianos (with lots of hands) exposes a harmonic sequence that systematically unfolds. As the work progresses, it becomes faster, louder and denser; but the rate of these changes are based on ‘just noticeable differences’.
