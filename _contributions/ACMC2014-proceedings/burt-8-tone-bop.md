Microtonal algorithmic hexachordal chord progression in 8 tone equal temperament made by drawing probability distributions by hand. In memoriam J K Randall, composer and deep musical thinker. Warren Burt is a microtonal composer based in Melbourne, as well as being one of the organizers of Melbourne 60x60.
