What does it mean to make music in a world where we have archaic brass instruments, plastics, and electronic cultures? Dale's been developing an approach to the trumpet with gardening irrigation, internal reeds, balloons, and footpumps. The result is no longer ‘an instrument’ but a hybrid system reminiscent of electronic music where valves function like switches for synthesizing and diffusing sounds. www.dalegorfinkel.com
