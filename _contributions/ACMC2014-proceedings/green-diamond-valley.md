Christina Green is a Melbourne-based musician writing and performing both folk/acoustic and contemporary classical music. Diamond Valley, which takes its name from the Diamond Valley area of Melbourne, is representative of her piano music, which combines jazz and classical elements. Images of places in the area, such as Eltham, were in the composer's mind while playing and working on the piece, and its slightly brooding, turbulent quality captures something of the energy of the area as experienced by her.
