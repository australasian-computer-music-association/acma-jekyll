The concept of TERRAUSTRALIS is 'a breath of the Ancient Spirits of this land', with the only sound sources of the piece being voices (afterwards processed/manipulated): the voice of the composer and the voices of birds recorded in the Bush. Written scores, electroacoustic creations, improvisation & experimental performance as well as cross-artform expressions, all play a vital role in Silvia Simons's output. Her work has been performed and broadcast in Australia, the USA & Europe.
