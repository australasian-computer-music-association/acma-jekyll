Tim Catlin is a a composer, instrument maker, composer and guitarist based in Melbourne. "Gossamer" is composed using Tim's microtonally tuned Vibrissa instruments which produce sounds acoustically using rosined gloves on the instruments metal rods. Besides some basic EQ no sound processing or effects were used.
