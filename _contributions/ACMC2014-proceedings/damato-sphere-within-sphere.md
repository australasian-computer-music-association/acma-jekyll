Sphere within a sphere is a short piece inspired by one of Arnaldo Pomodoro’s sculptures bearing the same name. It was moulded in 1991 and placed in the square in front of the United Nations Headquarters in New York. The piece describes a complex machine which reveals by degrees and only partially his working, by means of a gradual disclosure of its inner microstructures. Sound elements, both continuous and impulsive are elaborated, mixed and overlapped several times in order to give birth to a visionary sound landscape where a monolithic and smooth shape conceals deep stratified discontinuities like linked cogwheels.
