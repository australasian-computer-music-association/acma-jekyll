Memory Walk is a sound and video piece that aims to demonstrate and shape the counterpoint and harmony between perception, memory, and imagination in listening. It is one of a group of pieces that encourage the listener to critically engage with listening in the process of listening to them, made as part of the ongoing PhD project Listening Music. This paper discusses the aims of the piece, the project of which it forms a part, and the research process used to test its success at achieving its aims.
