Ernie Althoff started his forays into experimental music in the late 1970s. The digital editing suite has overcome the limitations of multi-track cassette technology, but one still needs to conceptualize the piece. Small percussion machines, clapping in a reverberant space, a pitch-shifted cricket - isn't it good to have an archive!
