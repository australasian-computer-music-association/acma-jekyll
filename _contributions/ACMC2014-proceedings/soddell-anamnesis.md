Anamnesis is "an effect of reminiscence in which a past situation or atmosphere is brought back to the listener's consciousness, provoked by a particular signal or sonic context." Sonic Experience - Jean- François Augoyard and Henry Torgue; Thembi Soddell (b.1980) is an Australian sound artist and electroacoustic composer. She is a PhD candidate at RMIT's School of Art where she is researching the articulation of psychological distress in sound practice.
