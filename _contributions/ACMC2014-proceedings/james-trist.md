Peter James was born in 1969 in Springvale, Melbourne, Australia and is a self-taught musician, artist and photographer. In 2005 Peter started the Iceage Productions imprint releasing music by Australian and international noise, experimental and electronic musicians. Trist was constructed on computer using found sounds. The age of concrete and steel is upon us, the urban sprawl envelops all in its path.
