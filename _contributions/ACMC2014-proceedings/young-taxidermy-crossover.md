Currently based in Berlin, David Young is a composer preoccupied with graphic notation, and was artistic director of Chamber Made Opera (2010-2013), Aphids (1994-2010) and the Next Wave Festival (2001-2002). 'Taxidermy Crossover': from the Greek for the arrangement of skin. How does music get under our skin, and what is the point of this crossover?
