Jim Barbour is an independent spatial audio researcher currently undertaking a PhD by Project at RMIT, investigating height in acoustic space. In former lives, Jim has been an audio engineer, music producer, radio production manager, theatre sound supervisor and academic but seeks a simpler life now. In this 60 second piece, Jim is exploring the sensation of virtual height generated by ever higher frequencies.
