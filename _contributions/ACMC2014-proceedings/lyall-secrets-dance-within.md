A 60 second dream sequence from "‘Edge’ for concert band, narrator and sound design", a deconstructed love story commissioned by the Melbourne Rainbow Band. Mark works at the Australian Catholic University as a lecturer in media theory. He plays the trumpet and euphonium, and is currently enjoying the company of several Victorian brass and concert bands.
