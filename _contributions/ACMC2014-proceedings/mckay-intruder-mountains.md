Intruder in the Mountains is created from field recordings in the Dandenong Ranges and explores ideas of space and location. As recording was taking place, an aeroplane intruded on the tranquil setting and shaped the narrative of the piece. Jaccob McKay is a multidisciplinary artist working with sound, photography and other media.
