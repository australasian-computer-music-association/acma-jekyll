–ecstasy was composed primarily out of developing ideas relating to mediality in music— observing the relationship between the work and its medium. Here, a medium I'd never previously engaged with (the soprano clarinet) is explored by way of its historic perception as a subordinate instrument, and the ever-present connotations of the "for instrument and electronics" continuum.
