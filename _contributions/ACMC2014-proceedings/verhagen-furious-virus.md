EPA is Darrin Verhagen’s noise project – the subject of his recent PhD and the birthplace of the RMIT Audiokinetic Experiments (AkE) lab. "Furious Virus" explores the recontextualization of an unstable Noise ground, transformed into a writhing figure through a trellis of rock drums.
