THAT consists of David Tolley, Ren Walters, and Dur-é Dara. We are all composers of each other’s works as well as our own. Collectively we function as the group 'THAT' and have done so for 20 years. We are a collaborative performance trio centred around improvisational music performance. The performance of these 3 works also marks the passing of David from this life. We feel that THAT's musical relationship continues in some indefinable manner, after Davids departure.
