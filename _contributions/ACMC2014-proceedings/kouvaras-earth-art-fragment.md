This piece is derived from my Bundanon Sonata for Violin and Piano (2011), written for, and performed here by, Marianne Rothschild (violin) and Glenn Riddle (piano). It was composed during an artist-in-residence stay at Bundanon, the Arthur & Yvonne Boyd bequest in NSW. Linda is a composer, musicologist and pianist, who holds a Senior Lectureship at the Melbourne Conservatorium of Music, Univ. of Melbourne.
