This short work depicts “Jinn”, literally meaning “hidden from sight.” In Islamic and Arabic culture, the Jinn are regarded as supernatural creatures inhabiting an unseen world in dimensions beyond the visible universe of humans. Catherine Sullivan is a Melbourne based composer currently completing studies at the University of Melbourne.
