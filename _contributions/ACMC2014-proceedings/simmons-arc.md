The "score" for this is a sculpture - a music box with a looped strip of paper, without holes. The melody is completely at the whim of the movement of the paper as it twists and turns through the mechanism. Adam Simmons is one of Australia's most eclectic woodwind players and composers, contributing to numerous ensembles and well known for the Adam Simmons Toy Band and Origami. More recently he has delved into sculpture as well as creating and directing the Festival of Slow Music.
