Gloss is inspired by a pen and ink drawing. An acoustic ensemble and live electronics interpret the drawing. The creative process that evolved from this simple idea explores facets of interactive performance that include 5 live instrumentalists, improvisation, spacialised sound exploration and visual elements. Brigid is an Australian composer, clarinet soloist, poly-media performance artist, video artist, visual artist, and educator. Brigid’s work also includes the exploration of acousmatic music, and composing multi-tasking performance works, live video footage, Bb/Bass Clarinet and live audio laptop configurations.
