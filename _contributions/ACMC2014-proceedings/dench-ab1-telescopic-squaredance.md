Written for Andrew Bernard, AB1 is one of the heretical bagatelles from my Phase Portraits piano collection. Performed by: Peter de Jager (piano) Recorded at: 3MBS. Composer biog: After 15 years living in Melbourne, the composer now lives in Ballarat; his music has been performed and broadcast internationally for more than 30 years. Performer biog: Peter de Jager loves exploring how music from all periods of time can co-exist, peacefully or otherwise; he is about to release his first solo album, featuring music from Bach to Chopin to Dench to Radiohead.
