A hose is unrolled and the gardener gets to work...but what was he thinking? (Samples were recorded and edited in Pro Tools LE as part of a Tropscore Composer submission in 2012, re-mixed in GarageBand for 60x60 in 2014). Born in Melbourne in 1955, I studied music at La Trobe University 1989 - 1995, composed for various Melbourne based choreographers 1995 - 2001, taught Instrumental Music in Regional Victorian Secondary Colleges 1998 - 2007, Grad Dip Ed at La Trobe University 2008, taught Classroom Music in Melbourne Primary Schools 2009 - 2014...
