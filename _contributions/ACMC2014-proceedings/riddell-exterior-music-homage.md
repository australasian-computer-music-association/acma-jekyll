Alistair Riddell thinks about music and occasionally composes it. This piece is a construction of fragments of a post improvisation discussion between Jim Randall, Rick Labante, Peter Polinsky (Architecture Masters students) and Alistair Riddell recorded in the MacAlpin Rehearsal Studio at the Woolworth Centre for Musical Studies at Princeton University on 28th March 1991. Jim Randall, who described himself as one of the “grandaddies of computer music" died 28th May 2014 at his home in Princeton. (Photo of J. K. Randall by Alistair Riddell)
