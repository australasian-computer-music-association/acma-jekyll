Heat is based on part of a field recording made early one morning down by the Yarra River during the oppressive heat of last summer then processed in various ways through Ross Bencina's Audiomulch progamme. Howard Dillon gained his music education from La Trobe University and is involved various musical activities as violinist, educator and composer.
