Digital composition systems shape the way a composer creates a composition. Several novel methods of digital composition have demonstrated that open-ended possibilities in the design of composition interfaces can be expanded on.

Generative non-linear processes have demonstrated their usefulness and ability to aid creative processes. This study investigates what tasks are effectively achieved and what problems arise when using two different types of digital composition systems, specifically focusing on linear and non-linear systems.
