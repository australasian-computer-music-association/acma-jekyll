Seadragons perform a stately dance together as part of their intricate social rituals. On the other hand, John Arthur Grant draws a curved line through performances at the Melbourne Town Hall, past the Christchurch Performing Arts Centre, on to a hangar at Broome airport, the beach at Chennai, and a hotel in Tehran. His music practice channels Thomas Tallis, Stravinsky, Skyhooks, and The Tubes.
