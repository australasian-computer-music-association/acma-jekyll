Air on a Broken String is a collation of the sound of a broken dan tranh string and its transformed versions. It is a fusion of minimalism and Bach in a digital multi-part invention. Le Tuan Hung creates new music with a touch of Vietnamese sensibility and colours and is an expert in Vietnamese musicology. He has been the producer and curator at the Australia Asia Foundation’s Sonic Gallery since 1994.
