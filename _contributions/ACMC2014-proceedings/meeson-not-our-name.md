Amidst the growing cacophony of Melbourne town can be heard voices of great diversity. ‘Not in our name’ is a lo fi glimpse of Melbourne town, some of its sounds, some of it’s spirit and some of its din. Catherine Meeson is a singer / songwriter electro acoustic composer from Melbourne with a passion for composing alternative soundscaped worlds. www.catherinemeeson.com
