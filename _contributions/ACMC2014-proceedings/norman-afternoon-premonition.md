Afternoon Premonition’ combines manipulated tea and bell sounds with a magpie out my back door in Mornington. While shakuhachi is my primary acoustic instrument, I occasionally manipulate sounds for use in my one-woman theatre shows using very basic software and processes to achieve the sound I want.
