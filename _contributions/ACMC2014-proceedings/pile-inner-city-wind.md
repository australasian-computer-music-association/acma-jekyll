Judy Pile is a freelance composer, performer and teacher whose passion for social justice and diverse musical interests have led her everywhere from the operatic stage to political street theatre. My new inner-Melbourne home is suffused with ‘wind music’, in the broadest sense of the expression. For this piece I used found sounds recorded in and around my flat to build a minimally processed collage of wind, birdsong, and my neighbour Fereshteh singing to and playing with her four-month-old baby.
