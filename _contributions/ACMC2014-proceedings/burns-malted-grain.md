A serialist piece, for piano and electronics. Angus "Acknack" Burns is a 28-year-old composer and film scorer, currently residing in Richmond, Victoria. He recently completed his Bachelor Degree in Composition at Box Hill Institute. Angus has focused most of his musical outpourings into serialism, atonality and twelve-tone techniques, however his repertoire spans many styles and genres, including classical, jazz, film music, rock, electronic music and remixing, sampling, and many more. For more information, visit: https://soundcloud.com/acknack/
