Whirling Knives was composed for a concert commemorating the 100th anniversary of the beginning of World War One. While it responds in part to present-day portrayals of wartime mythologies (particularly the Battle of Fromelles), it also calls into question the aesthetics of interpreting historical events using the contemporary mediums of computer and multi-speaker array. Digitally synthesised spatial musics have traditionally favoured abstract associations—my goal was to successfully navigate the contradictory aesthetics of the absolute and the thematic to create a piece that acknowledges the atrocities of World War One while retaining a degree of semantic independence.
