Roar uses the musical composition technique eco-structuralism to manipulate and blend the sonic structures within the roar of waves and the roar of a fire. Eco-structuralism is musical composition technique whereby field recordings are analysed to determine underlying structures, which are then used as musical constructs to create abstract musical works, thereby blending abstract music with natural sound.
