Johanna Selleck is a Melbourne-based composer and flautist. Her electronic piece, "Undone", was created in the computer program Sonar. Using samples from an earlier solo piano piece, "Undone" deconstructs the original work using techniques including different types of delay, reverb and reversal effects.
