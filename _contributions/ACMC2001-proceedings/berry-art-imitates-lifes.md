This artist's talk describes the undertaking of a project with a working name of ‘Vital Presence’ at a large government funded research institution somewhere in Japan. To maintain anonymity as requested, the artist is herein referred to as Frankenstein-san, his collaborators as Igor1-san, Igor2-san, Igor3-san, . . . Igor6-san respectively. The Institute shall be likewise referred to as The Bunker.
