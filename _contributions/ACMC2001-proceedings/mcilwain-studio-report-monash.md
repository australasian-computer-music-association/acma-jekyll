The Working With Sound project is currently under development in the School of Music – Conservatorium and the Centre for Electronic Media Arts at Monash University. This project involves the creation of an educational resource primarily aimed at undergraduate students studying: music, sound engineering, multimedia and computer science. Topics covered in Working With Sound include; basic physics of sound, sound synthesis, recording and mixing technology. The resource is designed in a selfpaced learning format that includes theoretical information integrated into practical exercises. It features a suite of software applications that can generate and process sound. This enables students to explore, in a practical way, the generation and manipulation of sound by the direct application of information with experimentation.
