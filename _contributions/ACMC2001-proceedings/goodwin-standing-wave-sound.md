This paper will discuss a standing wave sound sculpture which I created at Monash University in November last year. The term standing wave sound sculpture pertains to a sculpture using the materials of sound, which are the rapid vibrations of air particles. This differs from the more common definition of sound sculpture, which is a physical object that can create, or be used to create sound.
