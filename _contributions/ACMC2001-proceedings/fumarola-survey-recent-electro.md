A concert-talk featuring some recent cutting-edge electroacoustic and computer music pieces by Latinamerican composers covering different trends and approaches, be it aesthetical, technological, compositional, etc. There will be references to historical landmarks in Latinamerican ea/cm and how they influenced today's production.
