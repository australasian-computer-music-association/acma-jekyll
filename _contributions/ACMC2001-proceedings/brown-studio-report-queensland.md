The last 12 months have been a busy one for music at QUT. There have been numerous concerts and CDs created by students and staff, including Andy Arthurs' (HOD) compositions for the Queensland Theatre Company productions. Students continue to play regularly in Brisbane clubs and the alternative electronic music scene has grown rapidly with regular concerts at the new Powerhouse Centre for Live Arts and many events organised by Brisbane artists such as Andrew Kettle and Michael Norris in which staff and students of QUT regularly participate. We have been commissioned to do the web site sound design for this years Brisbane Festival of Music. The electronic music ensemble Simulation are touring to this conference and then to Melbourne for performances as part of our expanding emphasis on the integration of live performance and music technologies.
