This paper examines local activity in non-academic electronic music, proposing that this work can be analysed as, among other things, a form of media art practice. It is proposed that an engagement with audio media as such is a central feature of this work; specifically, that this work can be understood as an attempt to hear, and experience, audio media substrates. The notion of inframedia is proposed, standing for media practices which operate in this way, below the level of content. Continuities are demonstrated between this audio inframedia practice and visual and audiovisual inframedia forms.
