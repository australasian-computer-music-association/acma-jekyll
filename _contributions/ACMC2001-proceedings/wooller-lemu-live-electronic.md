This paper examines the software instrument “LEMu”, which is being developed by the authors at the Queensland University of Technology. In this paper we will answer the questions: “What is LEMu?”, “What is it designed to do?”, “How does it work?”, “What music does it produce?” and “What are some future developments of LEMu?”. Many types of intelligent instruments are emerging from both the academic and commercial worlds. For example there is the interactive algorithmic composition AutoGam, developed by Thierry Bachman and the Korg KARMA keyboard, developed by Stephen Kay. Presently the commercial dance scene lacks (to some extent) real time performance, creation and improvisation. LEMu encourages DJ’s and electronic musicians to explore these concepts within the context of the dance music scene.
