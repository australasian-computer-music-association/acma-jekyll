This short paper will consider the Oracular powers of Sound in the prediction and construction of imaginative Utopic (and Dystopic) futures - in effect a pre-technological form of Virtual Reality. I will discuss the traditional fusion of site, sculpture, speech and belief systems, which characterise Oracles and then focus upon the relationship of Sound to Political power and territorial conflict. The final section of the paper will examine the re-emergence of Sound as a spatialising metaphor and agent in Cinema and in immersive Virtual Reality systems.
