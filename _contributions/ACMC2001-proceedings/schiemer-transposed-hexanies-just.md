Just intonation tuning systems that have no tonal centres provide the starting point for this discussion on tuning associated with a series of works entitled Transposed Hexanies. The composer describes the origins of the hexany, the hexanies used in this these works, their transformation and extension through pitch multiplication and the connections between rhythms and tunings used in these works. The lecture will demonstrate how transposition extends the scope of the hexany. The transposition algorithm will be explained together with its ramifications for live performance using hand-held instruments capable of performing in tunings other than twelve equal divisions of the octave.
