An improvisation where ambient/drone guitar meets glitchy electronica. Minimalist electric guitar, micro-synths and Axoloti microcontroller are processed using analog and digital stompboxes and further mangled and augmented using Plogue Bidule modular audio software.

The performer “begin(s) anywhere” and relies on spontaneity and serendipity, pushing to to the edge of discomfort and vulnerability. Layers of asynchronous loops build to create complex and beautiful textures.

Performed in one take, no edits. Recorded on 19th June 2020.
