This work involves performer meta-control of three autonomous musical agents that employ the Gendynish algorithm (Brown 2019). In the spirit of post-digital aesthetics, the work exploits the materiality of Arduino-class microprocessors to produce sounds similar to Iannis Xenakis’ generative dynamic synthesis (Xenakis 1992). The work draws inspiration from Xenakis’s innovative use of probabilistic processes to explore the nature of digital sound making and the unfolding of compositional events. The work adds to this approach, real-time, parallel, semi-autonomous, evolutionary processes that shape the sound world over time and are guided by the musician during performance or rendering.

### References

Brown, Andrew R. 2019. “Gendynish: Stochastic Synthesis on the Arduino.” In Proceedings of the International Symposium on Electronic Art, 638–39. Gwangju, Korea: ISEA International

Xenakis, Iannis. 1971. Formalized Music: Thought and Mathematics in Composition. Bloomington: Indiana University Press.
