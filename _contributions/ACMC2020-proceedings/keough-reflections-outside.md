bio: Ben Keough is an experimental composer/improviser and amateur photographer/videographer currently living in Bellingen on the NSW Mid North Coast. A graduate of the ANU School of Music, his composition work is based around digital synthesis and generative sequencing techniques (using the Pure Data development environment), field recording and electroacoustic improvisation.
---
This work is part of an ongoing interest in the intersection of acoustic ecology, novel synthesis techniques and generative composition. It imitates and deconstructs the timbral, structural and textural aspects of natural sound environments using a combination of processed field recordings and self-playing/generative synthesizer patches developed in the Pure Data visual programming environment.
