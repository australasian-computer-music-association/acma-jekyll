*intro/exo* is a composition for a bespoke headset that allows the audience to experience extraordinary spatial and sonic phenomena. The headset consists of two loudspeakers, which sit on the ears like normal headphones, and two surface transducers that sit on the cheekbones of the wearer. These transducers vibrate the cheekbones and skull, allowing the experience of sounds seemingly emanating from within the head. The work investigates the creative and experiential affordances of the bespoke headset by placing the listener's head as the site of exploration, creating sounds that move around and inside the listener's body. The work employs binaural techniques to create hyper-realistic sonic environments, contrasted with the intimacy of the surface transducers. It is the second in a series of works that explore the creative potentials of these interfaces.
