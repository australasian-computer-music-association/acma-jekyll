*within these cells* (2023), for 4 speakers. When you are held captive for an indefinite amount of time, not being able to talk to others or even see the sunlight, one can only wonder what it does to a person's state of mind. The work explores this idea by expressing the paranoia and fear that a person feels as they become an inmate, living in an isolated cell block. Having to cope with post-traumatic stress disorder by themselves, alone, they slowly descend into insanity.
