*Xeno* is a multimedia work based on the intense, dense, and continuous twine between audio and video. 'Xeno', a Greek word indicating something extraneous, describes this work as it proposes a novel, uncommon, and subverted relationship between the two media. Breaking the rule that sonic events are always the consequence of physical actions, Xeno proposes an alien reality in which this relationship is altered, making it difficult to understand which of the two media is the cause or the consequence of the other. The meaning also refers to the rapidly, continuously mutating multimedia content as a whole.
