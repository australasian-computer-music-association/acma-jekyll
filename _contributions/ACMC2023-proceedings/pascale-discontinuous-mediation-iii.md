*Discontinuous Mediation III* is the third composition of this series. Unlike the first and second, which used pre-recorded guitar and clarinet sounds, here pre-recorded viola sounds are used as sonic material. As in the predecessors, the sounds were organized in a segmented way, forming a structure based on the composer's compositional procedure. This method explores the conceptual idea of a perceptually continuous world being interpreted by a discrete mediation.
