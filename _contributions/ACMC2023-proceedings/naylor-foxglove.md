Created from sounds of a tree isolated from its typical natural environment, *Foxglove* explores a heightened sense of scale resulting from extremely close recording techniques, reframing minuscule natural processes as functions of a great ever-growing entity. Here, almost imperceptible sounds are magnified, becoming part of a series of vast and enveloping spaces interior and exterior to the tree.
