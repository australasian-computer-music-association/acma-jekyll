51 instances of audio created with Ableton Live's Operator synthesizer, each recorded for 1.8 seconds at 133.33 bpm and organised in Live's Session View as looping Audio Clips. Follow Actions were used to generate random playback at various intervals. The original Audio Track was duplicated multiple times, and LFOs applied to the volume fader for each track to impart an additional degree of chance upon the composition.
