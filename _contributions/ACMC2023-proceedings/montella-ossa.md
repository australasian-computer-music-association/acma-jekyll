bio: Paolo Montella is an electroacoustic composer, multi-instrumentalist, and programmer. Field recording and radical improvisation practices are central to his aesthetic, and he focuses his research on the relationship between sound and source. He graduated in Electronic Music at the Naples Conservatory with M° Elio Martusciello. "In this world which we enter, appearing from a nowhere, and from which we disappear into a nowhere, Being and Appearing coincide" (Hannah Arendt). Field recording, as an operation determined by rituals, times, and techniques, releases the need to be artistically varied. In this work, field recording is grafted onto the practice of dozens of interviews conducted between June and December 2021 with women from small towns in southern Italy. Agata, an 11-year-old girl, shows us her field at the centre of tense and contradictory forces, between her being a child and wanting to become a woman.
