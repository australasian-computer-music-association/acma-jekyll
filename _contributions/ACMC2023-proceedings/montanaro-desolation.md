*Desolation* is an acousmatic composition made with digital and environmental sound materials. The title is inspired by the desolation inherent in a man who does not accept belonging to the social fabric he lives in because he does not recognize himself in it. Digital synthesis is obtained through subtractive synthesis processes: given a sound source rich in harmonics, it is filtered from a spectral point of view by "subtracting" frequency bands or single partials. This filtering develops during the temporal evolution of the signal, organized into three sound typologies (low-, mid-, and high-pitched) and rich in beats.
