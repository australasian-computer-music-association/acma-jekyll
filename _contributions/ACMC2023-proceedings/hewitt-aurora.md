*Aurora* explores the dormant Dionysian essence within each of us. As night descends, it awakens our primal instincts, an irresistible force that takes hold of us, bringing forth both chaos and transformative new order. This work invites the audience to contemplate the innate power and potential of embracing our wild and untamed nature. The work incorporates AI images made in collaboration with Midjourney.
