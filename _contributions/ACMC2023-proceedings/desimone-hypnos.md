Hypnos and Thanatos — Sleep and Death. Death mirrors Sleep, because it is the latter that interacts with life; it is life itself, while Death represents its mirror opposite. Here Hypnos is introduced; Thanatos can wait. Starting from sounds recorded on the lakeside of the town where the composer was born, he imagined what the soundscape will be in the future.
