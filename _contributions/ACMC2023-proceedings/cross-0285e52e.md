*0285e52e* is a data-driven composition created by combining over one thousand short field recordings recorded by the composer and arranged based upon their ranging characteristics of spectromorphological complexity. Edited using statistical analysis, this piece plays on the embellishment of spectral fluctuation, spatial motion, timbre, dynamic range and differences in recording quality.
