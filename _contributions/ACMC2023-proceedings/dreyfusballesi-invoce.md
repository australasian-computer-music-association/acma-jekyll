*InVoce* is a computer choral performance that delves into the expressive qualities of the human voice through the integration of a motion and gesture interface. This live performance involves an ensemble of smartphone musicians, each controlling an expressive vocal synthesizer, resulting in a collaborative and improvised exploration of melody and harmony. InVoce extends beyond the limitations of traditional vocal capabilities, allowing performers to augment their ordinary utterances and venture into new realms of vocal expression through nuanced, expressive gestural control.
