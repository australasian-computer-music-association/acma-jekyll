Synchronised Swamp is an artistic audio-video installation that simulates synthetic frog and insect-like choruses both audibly and spatially using a computer, multi-channel sound output and distributed speaker system. The software plays back several samples of a synthetic chorusing swamp creature per audio channel. Each sample is repeated periodically. A mathematical model is then used to bring the collectively sounding samples and visuals in and out of synchrony.
