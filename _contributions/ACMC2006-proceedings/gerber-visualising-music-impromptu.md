This paper discusses our experiments with a method of creating visual representations of music using a graphical library for Impromptu that emulates and builds on Logo’s turtle graphics. We explore the potential and limitations of this library for visualising music, and demonstrate some ways in which this simple system can be utilised to assist the musician by revealing musical structure.
