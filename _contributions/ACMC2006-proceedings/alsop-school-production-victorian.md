This report introduces the courses, studios and practise methods employed in teaching sound design skills in the School of Production, VCA. The courses offered are: Master of Sound Design by research, Graduate Diploma in Dramatic Arts (Sound Design) and sound design skills as part of the broader undergraduate program. The teaching methods range from: direct on line teaching, to be demonstrated; class room teaching in software/hardware systems (including Max/MSP/Jitter, ProTools, Logic, Audition, AudioMulch); practical collaborative workshops with other artists, (including in 2006 Phillippe Genty, Ronnie Burkett, DAS ARTS, Orygen/Beyond Blue, and Commonwealth Games/FestivalMelbourne 2006) and various VCA performances with local and international artists.
