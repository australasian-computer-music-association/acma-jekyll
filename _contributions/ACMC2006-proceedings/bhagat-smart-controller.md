My particular interest lies in spontaneity of music, especially in a concert situation. This paper talks about few ideas of implementing Mini MIDI controller in a live performance. One key limitation with laptop performance is interaction of a computer with a human player or vice versa. In an improvised performance this device would act as a source of communication. between man and a machine. The endless options it gives you by converting voltages into bytes and sending it across to multiple devices, opens up a whole new world of composition process either in a concert situation or in a studio environment.
