This paper presents some preliminary results from a detailed analysis of Denis Smalley’s Wind Chimes. The segregation-integration, assimilation-meaning (SIAM) framework previously developed by the author is applied to several sections of the work. Conclusions are drawn regarding the possible syntactical forces operating within the piece.
