Drum machines are these days as much a part of popular and contemporary music as the violin or piano were during the classical era. Although these machines have been around for excess of 30 years, they have not really evolved to any great extent and usually involve the often-tedious task of programming steps into patterns, which then need to be sequenced or switched in the correct order. My research has looked at furthering previous drum machine models, turning them into playable instruments for use in live improvisation rather than machines that have to be pre-programmed, pre-prepared and have little or no human interaction The model described here features GUI and MIDI interface functionality for human control and uses probabilities to generate ongoing variation. Due to this model’s functionality it has proven to work extremely well in improvised musical situations and also to successfully mimic the beats and techniques of a human drummer.
