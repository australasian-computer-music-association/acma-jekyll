Sounds From Level Four is a new media work developed by the Adelaide visual arts collective Shoot, where I collaborated as a sound artist. It is a work sponsored primarily by the Adelaide City Council and VAF Sound Research, which was first exhibited in the Adelaide Museum exhibition space from February 24th to March 17th as part of the 2006 Adelaide Fringe Festival. In this presentation, I will elaborate upon the collaborative creative process between myself and the Shoot Collective which led to the finished product.
