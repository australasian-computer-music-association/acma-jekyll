My name is Darren Curtis, I am a honours student at Adelaide University in Music Technology. I am very interested in using this device to convert light into sonic frequencies.

This conversion from light into sound is a valid research tool for my work in transposing DNA spectral ranges of light into corresponding frequency ranges of sound.
