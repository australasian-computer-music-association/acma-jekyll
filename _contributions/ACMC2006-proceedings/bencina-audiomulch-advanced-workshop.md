AudioMulch is software for electronic music performance and composition available for Windows PCs from http://www.audiomulch.com/ In July 2006 AudioMulch's creator Ross Bencina will conduct an advanced workshop as part of ACMC06. The workshop will be presented in a flexible format allowing attendees to ask questions relevant to their own use of the software. The workshop will focus on advanced applications including: performance signal processing patches, MIDI modulation and mapping, The Metasurface, and network synchronisation. Ross will disassemble and explain a number of his own performance patches to give insight into the capabilities of this flexible live performance and composition environment. Explanations of some of the less simple contraptions (for example SouthPole and LiveLooper) will be given according to the level and interest of the audience. The workshop is intended for people who have some experience with the AudioMulch software. At a minimum attendees are expected to have downloaded the software from www.audiomulch.com and familiarised themselves with its basic operation. Attendees are invited to bring their own laptops and AudioMulch patches to discuss. Computers will also be available at the workshop and attendees are requested to please bring their own headphones. A discount will be offered to workshop attendees wishing to purchase AudioMulch on the day.
