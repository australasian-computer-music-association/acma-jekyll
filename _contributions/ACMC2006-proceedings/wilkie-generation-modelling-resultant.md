This report explores the auditory illusion of Resultant Tones through the collation, generation and analysis of documented theoretical methodologies that arithmetically determine resultant tone frequencies. Elements investigated include the analysis of frequency relationships within the resultant tone generation and similar arithmetically determined frequencies present in the physical generation of sound; and the manipulation of physical parameters for emphasising the perception of resultant tones.
