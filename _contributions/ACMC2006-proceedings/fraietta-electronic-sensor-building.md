This one day workshop will give artists a primer into the electronics for developing their own gestural controller or installation through a “hands on” workshop. Participants will learn the basics of building voltage divider and switch type sensors by building and testing circuits on a breadboard. Electronic components covered are resistors, switches, transistors, diodes, and relays. Additionally, a section on making your instrument more reliable with tips on soldering, wire selection and stress relief will be provided.
