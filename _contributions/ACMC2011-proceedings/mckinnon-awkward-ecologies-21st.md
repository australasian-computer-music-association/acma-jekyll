An examination of those forms of music that are genealogically related to musique concrète – identified variously as acousmatic music, electroacoustic music, and sound-based music – which makes the core argument that the mastery of sound matter, and by extension of nature, is now extensive enough that the modes of intention and reception underpinning these forms of music, are secondary to a particular mode: the technological. The talk unfolds this argument as a necessary preamble to my conclusion, which sketches a relationship between this argument and the notions of ecology and a 21st century baroque.
