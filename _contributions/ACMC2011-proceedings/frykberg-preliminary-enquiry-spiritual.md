This paper is a consequence of an internet call I put out in January 2011, through the CEC, for electroacoustic music of a sacred/spiritual/religious nature, to be used for my radio show. It is a preliminary response to the surprisingly large number of e-mails I received. It attempts to do the following: To categorise some of the almost one hundred e-mail responses in terms of country and general type of music; to quote six of the composers who e-mailed me; to list future possible areas of enquiry in the area; to let you hear excerpts from the following works: Requiem, Georges Forget; Ikaro, Gary Kendall; Homo Religiosus, Eldad Tsabary.
