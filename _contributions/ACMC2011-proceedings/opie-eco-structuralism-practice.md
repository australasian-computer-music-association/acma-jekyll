The eco-structural composition technique uses natural and organic sound source materials at the beginning of a composition project with the intention of imbuing composed music with a natural or organic structure. This paper will describe a case study exploration of an eco-structural musical work and the process of composing it, from the source material to the finished work. The paper concludes by discussing the effectiveness of the process and how it could be improved for future musical compositions.
