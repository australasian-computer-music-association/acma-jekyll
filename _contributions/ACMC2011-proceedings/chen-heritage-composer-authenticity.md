In seeking an artistic voice and identity, I am interested in applying sounds that relate to my heritage as the foundation of musical composition. The term heritage denotes “valued objects and qualities such as cultural, traditions… that have been passed down from the previous generations; a special or individual possession” (New Oxford Dictionary, 2010). Heritage encompasses the cultural-inheritance and the life-experience of an individual. It is associated with an individual intrinsically and plays a direct part in authenticity of a creative work. I found in the domain of electroacoustic music, the composer is offered the opportunity to obtain sounds that relate to him or her on a more personal level. For example, the compositional materials can be sounds from the composer’s family, childhood, special events, memories, and sounds from the habitual lands that are meaningful to the composer.
