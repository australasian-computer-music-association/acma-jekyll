My artist talk presents the prototype for the installation of a networked public sound sculpture planned for the Newcastle central business district in late 2011. The project is supported by the Newcastle City Centre Committee who, along with the organisation LiveSites, are seeking to install a range of projects over the coming year and are actively exploring strategies for interactive and innovative projects in the public domain.
