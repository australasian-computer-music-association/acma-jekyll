The paper describes the E-Suling, an Indonesian hyperinstrument modified with sensors and a microchip. We describe the software and hardware designs in a variety of different languages and frameworks. We also discuss how the E-Suling is used in composition and performance to extend new techniques to traditional Indonesian music.
