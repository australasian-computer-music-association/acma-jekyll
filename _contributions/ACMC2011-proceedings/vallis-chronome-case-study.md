While the Monome and its variants have created a large user community (and a variety of custom applications) the devices do not afford performers direct access to continuous control data from either the buttons or the LEDs. With the addition of continuous control, the Chronome provides a new open-source iteration of the Monome that is more expressive and organic to perform with. This increased control is provided through the addition of pressure control via the buttons, and multicolour support for the LEDs. By increasing the level of control, but keeping a familiar instrument design, the authors aim to facilitate an increase in the virtuosic potential afforded by the Monome family of devices. With this in mind, the authors have developed several new applications that utilize the Chronome’s new functionality, and provide increased levels of visual feedback, while taking advantage of the continuously variable “aftertouch” from the pressure buttons.
