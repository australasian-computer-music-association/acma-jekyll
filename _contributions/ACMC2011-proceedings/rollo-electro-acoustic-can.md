This article examines the possibility of composing electro-acoustic music and integrating traditional Māori waiata/ mōteatea and taonga pūoro. The focus will be on generating a model for composing new works and proving that electro-acoustic can enhance Māori music. The findings of a current PhD research will be highlighted along with ‘work in progress’ that will give support to this article.
