This paper outlines my PhD project which draws together current and new music practises by writing music that combines live electronic and acoustic performers with new technology in virtual and real performance spaces. The investigation is inspired by Weinberg’s (2005) Interconnected Music Networks and details the aesthetic and creative issues that are considered when collaborating with geographically dispersed musicians. This is an interesting area to research in due to its wide focus and the rapid growing interest in the area of Networked Music. The aim of the project is to develop awareness of new performance spaces while creating music suited to this genre that fulfils the composer’s aesthetic and practical considerations while using a wide range of sound and instrument sources.
