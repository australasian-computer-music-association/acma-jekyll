This paper presents details about the design of specific sound cues from four compositions, illustrating the artists’ efforts in exteriorising into sound aspects of human experience that correlate with concepts of the organic. I approach my enquiry with awareness of the value that those experiences entail, and by responding to my listening with a reflective attitude and empathy for the expressions rendered in these pieces.
