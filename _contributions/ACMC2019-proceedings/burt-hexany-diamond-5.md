“Hexany Diamond 5 7 11 13” is a 10-minute long fixed-media composition made with Wide Blue Sound’s “Orbit” sample-playback synthesizer, living inside Native Instrument’s Kontakt sampler. The piece is tuned in a scale derived from the work of Ervin Wilson, a 19 note scale called a Hexany Diamond, based on harmonics 5, 7, 11, and 13 (Narushima 2017). This paper discusses the software used for the composition, the patch, methods of performance, and the structure of the composition.
