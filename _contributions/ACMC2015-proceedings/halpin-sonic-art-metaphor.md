The author examines and explores the use of soundscape elements, mimetic sound-identities and spectromorphology to form metaphor and myth. A metaphor creates new links between otherwise distinct conceptual domains (Landy, 1991). Mythology is a vital feature of every culture. Myths are specific accounts in a time and place that is unspecified but which is understood as existing apart from ordinary human experience.
