Amplitude for vibraphone, tam-tam and pre-recorded electronics is entirely based on the physical-acoustic properties of various pre-recorded tam-tam sounds. Each is subjected to acoustic analysis with the partials present used to create synthetic sounds in the electronic component that closely imitate the original sounds. These partials also form the basis of the pitch material used in the vibraphone, which metaphorically re-enforces and amplifies the electronic component, synthesising entirely new tone colours.
