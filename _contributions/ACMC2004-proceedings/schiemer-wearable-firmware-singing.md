This paper discusses a design for a purpose-built interface that a wearer uses for controlling sound through movement. It presents work done so far on a mobile sound installation called the Singing Jacket and looks at ways in which the design can benefit from new developments in microcontroller technology. Several different microcontrollers are reviewed along with additional peripherals used for sensing movement and creating sound. These developments are presented against a backdrop of problems affecting musicians whose creative output is intimately bound up with changes that have been brought about by the ongoing development of electronic technology.
