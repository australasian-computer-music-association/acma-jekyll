This paper will discuss the efficiency of a software designed specifically for the creation and generation of variable audio loops. Thus, while proposing a new computer tool for musical expression linked to a specific compositional process (looping), I wanted to explore and personalise a compositional language that would integrate a new form of expression in the professional music world. The development of the software Quadraloop gave me the opportunity to explore a new way to create music. Rather than using tools yielded by existing musical traditions, I created a link with these traditions through technical research and programming. By giving emphasis on a specific parameter, it was easier to develop a unique graphical environment to explore new musical possibilities.
