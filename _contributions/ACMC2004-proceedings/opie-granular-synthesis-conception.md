There are a multitude of computer programs available to perform various tasks using granular synthesis. These programs were not spontaneous creations; they grew from an intellectual tree of knowledge that has been slowly developing for over 200 years. Inside each program resides the ideas of Jean-Baptiste Joseph Fourier, Albert Einstein, Erwin Schrödinger, Dennis Gabor, Iannis Xenakis, Curtis Roads, Barry Truax and others. These composers and scientists are the ghosts in the granular synthesis machine.
