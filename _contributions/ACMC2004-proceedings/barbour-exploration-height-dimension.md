Multi-channel surround sound audio with added height loudspeakers offers a richer perceptual experience than traditional stereo or 5.1 reproduction, recreating truly three-dimensional aural soundfields. Based on human auditory perception, surround sound technology may assist the exploration of acoustic space as an expressive dimension.
