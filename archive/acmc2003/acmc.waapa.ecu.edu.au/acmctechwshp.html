TECHNOLOGY WORKSHOPS

Andrew Brown: Jmusic Workshop
Tuesday July 8, 2.00-4.00 pm
WAAPA@ECU MIDI Lab 1.220a
jMusic is a project designed to provide composers and software developers with a library of compositional and audio processing tools. It provides a solid framework for computer-assisted composition in Java, and is also used for generative music, instrument building, interactive performance, and music analysis.
Blisters: Interactive-electroacoustic instruments
Wednesday July 9, 2.00-4.00 pm
WAAPA@ECU Room ES2 1.210
Members of Blisters demonstrate and discuss their work building and performing with their own Electro-acoustic Instruments. Sensor systems such as the STEIM SensorLab and Infusion Systems I-Cube will be discussed as well as the human/machine interface and the evolving performance practice of interactive electronic music.
Gil Wienberg: HyperScore Workshops
Thursday July 10 2.00-4.00 pm
Friday July 11 2.00-4.00 pmWAAPA@ECU PC Lab 16.236
Hyperscore is an application introducing children and non-musicians to musical composition and creativity in an intuitive and dynamic way. The Hyperscore system automatically realizes a full composition from a graphical representation, allowing individuals with no musical training to create professional pieces.
