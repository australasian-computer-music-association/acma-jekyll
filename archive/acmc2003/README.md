# ACMC 2003 (Converging Technologies): archived sources

Copies of web pages that the 2003 proceedings entries and conference page rely on,
kept here because the originals are gone (issue #29, issue #60). Files are stored
byte for byte as found, under their original host and path.

## The conference website, `acmc.waapa.ecu.edu.au`

Fetched from the Wayback Machine on 1 November 2026 with the `id_` URL form (the
page as served, without the Wayback toolbar). The site was cited as the conference
website in Gordon Monro's report (Chroma 34, p. 11). Its images and the per-paper
PDFs it linked are not copied; only the keynote and Fraietta PDFs were ever
captured, and they are already in `proceedings/ACMC2003-proceedings/`.

- **`acmconfdetail.html`** is the conference timetable (sessions I–VIII with
  times and rooms, the three concerts, the keynote and closing performance). It
  was captured after the conference and differs in several times from the ACMA
  page below; the entries use this one.
- **`acmcperfdetail.html`** ("SOUNDS@ACMC03") lists the works in the three
  conference concerts, the four e\*mergence concerts at PICA (9–12 July) and the
  Installations@ECU. The 2003 creative-work entries come from it.
- **`acmconfpapers.html`** lists the keynote, papers, artist talks and studio
  reports under section headings, each linked to a per-paper PDF
  named by its page range (e.g. `036-042Fraietta.pdf`).
- Most pages declare `charset=macintosh` (Mac Roman), so curly quotes and dashes
  look wrong if read as Latin-1 or UTF-8.

## The ACMA website, `www.acma.asn.au`

| File | Original URL | Source | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `www.acma.asn.au/ACMC03.html` | http://www.acma.asn.au/ACMC03.html | ACMA cPanel backup of 21 October 2022, `public_html/ACMC03.html` (file dated 15 June 2005) | `1e5f544adb678892006ae608d5fbb3b1bc67cc93635802aa8ade1a7b7c87b13b` |

ACMA's own page for the conference: theme, visiting artists, fees, the
programme with composer lists for the three concerts, workshops and the
e\*mergence series. The Wayback Machine has captures of the same URL from April
2003 onwards. This copy is from the backup, not the Wayback Machine. Its
timetable is an earlier draft of the conference site's: several times differ.
Both timetables name Waters among Concert I's composers, while the performance
page and Monro's report put his two works in Concert II.
